# Creative Face & Festival Makeup — From First Brushstroke to Artistic Designs

Paint flowers, butterflies, festival looks and fantasy faces, starting from your very first
brushstroke. Short lessons, cheap skin-safe materials and printable practice faces mean you need no
experience, no art skills and no model.

## Who this course is for

- Complete beginners. You don't need to be able to draw, and you don't need any makeup experience.
- People on a small budget. Every material comes with cheaper, easy-to-find substitutes and what a
  substitute must be able to do.
- People with nobody to practice on. You practice on paper and printed face templates first, then
  on your own hand and arm, then on your own face in a mirror.

## How it works

- **Learning by doing.** Every lesson ends in something you paint. Reading takes a few minutes;
  painting takes most of the time.
- **Pictures first.** Every design is shown step by step: Step 1 → Step 2 → Step 3 → Done. You can
  follow the pictures even if your English is limited.
- **Short and modular.** Stop after any level and you already have a real skill:
  - **Level 1 — Creative Face Painting:** tools and safety, brush control, color and symmetry, and
    your first designs (flowers, stars, butterflies, rainbows, animals, hero masks).
  - **Level 2 — Festival & Trance Makeup:** bold color, paint plus makeup, glitter, gems, neon and
    UV, patterns and complete festival looks.
  - **Level 3 — Fantasy & Artistic Makeup:** advanced blends, 3D effects, illusions, masks, fantasy
    characters and your own original designs.
  - **Optional Level 4 — Body Art:** take the same skills to hands, arms and shoulders.
- **Projects.** Thirteen step-by-step projects, from a rainbow cheek to your own original face design.
- **Printable practice faces.** Front face, face map, half faces, eye area, hand and arm and upper
  body, in A4 and US Letter, in every module download. Tip: slide a template into a clear sheet
  protector, paint on the plastic, wipe and repeat.
- **Quizzes.** A short multiple-choice quiz after every lesson and module checks the decisions that
  matter: which product is safe where, why a gradient is streaky, where a design goes on the face.

## Safety first

> [!WARNING]
> Use only products made for skin, and keep anything not approved for the eye area away from the
> eyes. Patch-test new products and stop if skin reacts. Never use craft paint, markers, craft
> glitter or household glue on skin.

The [safety guide](references/safety.md) has all the rules in one page, and the
[materials guide](references/materials.md) explains what to buy and what to use instead.

## What you need to start

A small set of water-activated face paints with white and black, one pointed round brush, one flat
brush, a few makeup sponges, two cups of water, a white plate and some baby wipes. Lesson 01.1 helps
you choose, or substitute, every item.
