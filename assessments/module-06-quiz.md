# Module 6 quiz

This quiz checks Module 6: telling cosmetic, bio and craft glitter apart, making glitter stay put and
taking it off, choosing skin-safe adhesives and placing face gems, and keeping neon, UV and glow paint
in the right places. The questions are short situations: you choose a product, decide where something
goes on the face, or work out what went wrong. You need 70% to pass.
