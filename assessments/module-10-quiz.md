# Module 10 quiz

This optional quiz goes with the optional body art module; the core course is complete without it.
It checks the decisions behind painting the body: scaling a face design up and re-planning it for the
arm and shoulder, painting wraps and bracelets on your own hand and arm, placing designs with the
collarbones and the center line, keeping paint off clothes and bends, and using the same safety
rules as on the face. Every question is a short real-life situation. You need 70% to pass, and you
can retake it.
