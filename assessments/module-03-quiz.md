# Module 3 quiz

This quiz checks the color and placement decisions you make when you paint: mixing clean colors from
a few, building a smooth sponge gradient, making a shape pop with outlines and highlights, placing a
symmetrical design with the face map, and choosing a palette that works on the skin you are painting.
Every question is a short real-life situation. You need 70% to pass, and you can retake it.
