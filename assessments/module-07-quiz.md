# Module 7 quiz

This quiz checks the decisions behind festival patterns and a complete look: keeping dots even and
mandalas symmetrical, painting straight geometric lines on a curved face, making waves and swirls
that flow with the face and stay readable, and planning, painting, setting, photographing and
removing a full festival look safely. Every question is a short real-life situation. You need 70% to
pass, and you can retake it.
