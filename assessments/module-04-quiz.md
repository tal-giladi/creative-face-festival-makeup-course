# Module 4 quiz

This quiz checks the first designs from Module 4: flowers and leaves, stars, hearts and sparkles,
the butterfly, rainbows and clouds, cat and tiger faces, and hero masks and crowns. The questions are
short situations: you decide where a design goes, which product is safe, or what went wrong and how
to fix it. You need 70% to pass.
