# Module 9 quiz

This quiz checks the decisions behind fantasy characters and masks: turning one idea into a few
key features, a palette and thumbnails, choosing the design that reads from a distance, planning and
shading a masquerade mask that keeps the eyes safe, building a character from features, a color
story and one texture, choosing themes respectfully, and improving your own designs with references,
versions, a self-check and a portfolio. Every question is a short real-life situation. You need 70%
to pass, and you can retake it.
