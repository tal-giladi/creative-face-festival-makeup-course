# Module 2 quiz

This quiz checks the brush control skills from Module 2: holding and loading the brush, the pressure
stroke, teardrops and petals, swirls and spirals, dots and dot trails, and taking all of them from
paper to your own skin. The questions are short situations: you choose what to do or what went
wrong. You need 70% to pass.
