# Module 1 quiz

This quiz checks the decisions you make before, during and after painting: choosing a safe kit and
substitutes, reading labels for skin and eye-area use, patch testing and hygiene, mixing paint to the
right consistency, and taking paint off and cleaning your tools. Every question is a short real-life
situation. You need 70% to pass, and you can retake it.
