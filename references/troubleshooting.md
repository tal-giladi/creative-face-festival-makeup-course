# Fix-it guide

Find what you see in the left column, then try the fix. The lesson link explains it fully. If skin
stings, itches, swells or turns red, stop and take the paint off first: see the
[safety guide](safety.md). Problems are grouped by the module where the fix is taught.

## Module 1: Tools, Materials and Safety

| What you see | Likely cause | Try this | Lesson |
|---|---|---|---|
| Using craft or acrylic paint on skin | it looks like face paint and costs less | use only paint sold as face or body paint | [01.1](../lessons/module-01/lesson-01.md) |
| Lines are fuzzy or doubled | the round brush tip is split | use it for filling; use a brush that springs to one point for lines | [01.1](../lessons/module-01/lesson-01.md) |
