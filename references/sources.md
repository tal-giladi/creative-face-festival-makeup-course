# Sources

The safety and product guidance in this course was checked against these sources on 7 October 2026.
Rules and products change: when a label or a newer official page disagrees with the course, follow the
label and the official page.

## United States (FDA)

- [Novelty Makeup](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetic-products/novelty-makeup) — face paint and
  Halloween makeup; fluorescent colors are not allowed near the eyes; patch-test on the arm.
- [Color Additives and Cosmetics: Fact Sheet](https://www.fda.gov/industry/color-additives/color-additives-and-cosmetics-fact-sheet)
  — colors must be approved for the intended use; eye-area rules; glow-in-the-dark color.
- [Color Additives Permitted for Use in Cosmetics](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetic-ingredient-names/color-additives-permitted-use-cosmetics)
  — the tables of permitted colors and their allowed uses.
- [21 CFR 73.2995, Luminescent zinc sulfide](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/21/73.2995) — glow
  color limits and the "Do not use in the area of the eye" label.
- [Halloween Safety Tips](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/halloween-safety-tips-costumes-candy-and-colored-contact-lenses)
  — test makeup first; decorative contact lenses.
- [Eye Cosmetic Safety](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetic-products/eye-cosmetic-safety) — don't
  share eye cosmetics; keep applicators clean.
- [Temporary Tattoos, Henna/Mehndi, and "Black Henna"](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetic-products/temporary-tattoos-hennamehndi-and-black-henna-fact-sheet)
  — PPD in "black henna".
- [Kohl, Kajal, Surma … Beware of Lead Poisoning](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetic-products/kohl-kajal-al-kahal-surma-tiro-tozali-or-kwalli-any-name-beware-lead-poisoning)

## European Union and United Kingdom

- [Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009 on cosmetic products](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2009/1223/oj)
  and [Annex IV, list of colorants allowed](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1223/annex/IV)
  — each colorant's allowed uses, e.g. "not to be used in eye products".
- [CosIng, the EU cosmetic ingredient database](https://ec.europa.eu/growth/tools-databases/cosing/)
  — look up any ingredient or CI color number.
- [Commission Regulation (EU) 2023/2055, restriction of microplastics](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/chemicals/reach/restrictions/commission-regulation-eu-20232055-restriction-microplastics-intentionally-added-products_en)
  — loose plastic glitter ban and the transition periods for cosmetics.
- [Contact dermatitis, NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/contact-dermatitis/)
