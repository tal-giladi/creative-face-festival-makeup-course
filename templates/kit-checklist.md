# Kit checklist

Pack this before you paint. Items in the second list are only needed from Level 2.

## Every time

- [ ] Face paints (patch-tested), including white and black
- [ ] Round brush (pointed) and flat or filbert brush
- [ ] Sponges: one per person, plus spares
- [ ] Two water cups (rinse and clean) and water
- [ ] White palette, plate or lid
- [ ] Baby wipes (fragrance-free), paper towels, cotton pads
- [ ] Mild soap or gentle cleanser for removal
- [ ] Mirror, hairband or clips, old T-shirt or towel
- [ ] Your design plan or printed face map

## Festival extras

- [ ] Cosmetic glitter and a skin-safe glitter base
- [ ] Face gems and a skin-safe adhesive (latex-free if needed)
- [ ] UV face paint (label checked: not near the eyes) and a small UV torch
- [ ] Setting spray or translucent powder
- [ ] Oil-based cleanser and eye-safe remover
- [ ] A small bag for used sponges and wipes
