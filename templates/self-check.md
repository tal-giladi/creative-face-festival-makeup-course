# Self-check

Look at your finished design in a mirror or a photo, from about one meter away, then close up.
Tick what is true. Then pick **one** box you could not tick and make that your goal next time.

## From one meter away

- [ ] I can tell what it is in one second.
- [ ] The two sides match (if it is meant to be symmetrical).
- [ ] It sits where I planned on the face map (not too low, not off to one side).
- [ ] There is a clear main color and the colors look good together.

## Close up

- [ ] Lines are smooth and end in a point or a clean end, not a blob.
- [ ] The base color is even (no streaks, no patches you can see through).
- [ ] Outlines are thin and black only where they help the shape.
- [ ] White highlights are small and all on the same side.
- [ ] No paint, glitter or gems where they shouldn't be (eyes, lash line, lips).

## After wearing it

- [ ] It lasted as long as I needed.
- [ ] My skin felt fine during and after, and it came off easily.
