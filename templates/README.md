# Templates

Pages to copy into a notebook or print. The printable practice faces (front face, face map, half
faces, eye area, hand and arm, upper body, swatch card) are in every module download; any lesson's
link to `labs/` downloads them.

- [Design plan](design-plan.md) — plan a face design before you paint: idea, colors, face map, order of work.
- [Patch test log](patch-test-log.md) — record every new product you test and how your skin reacted.
- [Self-check](self-check.md) — check any finished design in two minutes and choose one thing to improve.
- [Kit checklist](kit-checklist.md) — what to pack before you paint, at home or at a festival.
