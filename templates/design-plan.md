## 4. Order of work

Big to small, light to dark, wet to dry. Tick as you go:

- [ ] Sponge base and gradients
- [ ] Main shapes with the flat or round brush
- [ ] Details and line work
- [ ] Black outlines
- [ ] White highlights and dots
- [ ] Glitter and gems (last)

## 5. Products check

- [ ] Every product is sold for skin and has been patch-tested
- [ ] Eye-area parts use only eye-safe products
- [ ] Remover ready
