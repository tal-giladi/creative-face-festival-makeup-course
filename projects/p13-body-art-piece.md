# Project 13 · Arm-and-shoulder piece (optional)

Intermediate · 75 minutes. An optional project for Module 10 (the core course is complete without
it): one flowing flower vine that starts with a big flower on the shoulder cap, runs along the
collarbone, spirals down the arm and ends in a leaf bracelet at the wrist. It combines lessons
[10.1](../lessons/module-10/lesson-01.md), [10.2](../lessons/module-10/lesson-02.md) and
[10.3](../lessons/module-10/lesson-03.md).

## Finished look

![Project 13 finished, on tan skin: a big purple flower on the shoulder cap with a soft purple glow, a green vine with leaves along the collarbone that ends in a curl before the center line, a second vine that spirals down the upper arm and forearm, leaving one edge and coming back on the other, two small magenta flowers on the vine, a leaf bracelet at the wrist, thin black outlines, white highlights and white dot trails. A gray-blue top covers the chest.](../assets/p13-arm-shoulder.svg)

What makes it work: one **flow line** carries the whole piece, from the shoulder cap to the wrist, so
it reads as one design and not as separate stickers. The biggest motif sits on a landmark (the
shoulder cap), the vine **wraps** around the arm instead of running straight down it, nothing crosses
the inside of the elbow, and the collarbone vine stops before the center line and above the neckline.

## Materials

> [!WARNING]
> Only patch-tested face and body paint ([lesson 01.3](../lessons/module-01/lesson-03.md)), never
> craft paint, acrylics or markers. Don't paint over cuts, rashes or sunburned skin. Paint only skin
> you are comfortable showing, keep the design off straps and necklines, and wash your hands before
> eating if your hand is painted.

- Face and body paint: purple, magenta, green, a light yellow-green, yellow, black and white (or your
  own colors that contrast with your skin).
- A whole sponge for the glow behind the flower.
- A round brush size 4-6 for the petals, vines and leaves; a size 1-2 round or liner for outlines,
  veins and highlights; a dotting tool.
- A light face paint or cosmetic pencil for the lead lines.
- A big mirror, your phone camera, a rolled towel for the arm, an old top.
- Mild soap and a gentle oil cleanser for the shower afterwards.
- The `arm-shoulder-piece` sheet from [labs/module-10](../labs/module-10/) (or `upper-body` and
  `hand-arm` from [labs/common](../labs/common/)).

Substitutes for everything are in [Materials and substitutes](../references/materials.md).

## Step by step

![Project 13 in five steps, on tan skin. 1: thin white lead lines: a circle on the shoulder cap, a line along the collarbone, a vine down the arm and a curve around the wrist. 2: the purple flower on the shoulder cap over a soft purple glow. 3: green vines with leaves along the collarbone and down the arm. 4: the leaf bracelet at the wrist and two small magenta flowers on the arm vine. Done: thin black outlines on the flowers, light green veins, white highlights and white dot trails.](../assets/p13-arm-shoulder-steps.svg)

Paint it on your non-dominant arm and shoulder, so your painting hand reaches across the body.

1. **Plan and lead lines.** Put on an old top. In the mirror, sketch a circle on the shoulder cap, a
   line along the collarbone that stops before the center line, a vine that spirals down the arm
   (crossing to the back of the arm above and below the elbow, never over its inside), and a curve
   all the way round the wrist ([lesson 10.1](../lessons/module-10/lesson-01.md)).
2. **Shoulder flower.** Sponge a soft purple glow on the shoulder cap, then paint five big petals with
   the round brush, tails to the center, and a yellow center
   ([lesson 04.1](../lessons/module-04/lesson-01.md)).
3. **Vines and leaves.** Paint the collarbone vine with a curl at its end. Then rest the arm on the
   rolled towel and paint the arm vine on the part facing you, turning the arm to continue it round
   ([lesson 10.2](../lessons/module-10/lesson-02.md)). Leaves on alternate sides, pointing the same
   way.
4. **Bracelet and buds.** Close the leaf bracelet just above the wrist bone in three turns of the
   arm. Add two small magenta flowers on the arm vine.
5. **Details.** Thin black outlines on the flowers, light veins in the leaves, white highlights on
   the petals and dot trails along the vines. Check from two meters away in the mirror.
6. **Dry, set, dress.** Let it dry for several minutes, set it lightly
   ([lesson 05.2](../lessons/module-05/lesson-02.md)), then put on your top.
7. **Removal later:** in the shower with warm water, mild soap and an oil cleanser, no scrubbing.

## Practice version

On the `arm-shoulder-piece` sheet in a clear sheet protector, trace the whole piece once in the
colors above, then paint it again on a fresh sheet in your own palette. Then paint the bracelet and a
short piece of the arm vine on your forearm, and the flower with the collarbone vine on your shoulder,
before you try the full piece.

## Variations

- **Ocean wrap:** a big shell or starfish on the shoulder cap, a wave band
  ([lesson 07.3](../lessons/module-07/lesson-03.md)) that spirals down the arm in blues and teals, and
  a dot-and-teardrop bracelet.
- **Festival sleeve:** a dot mandala on the shoulder cap ([lesson 07.1](../lessons/module-07/lesson-01.md)),
  a geometric band down the outer arm, and a little cosmetic glitter on the shoulder in a gel.
- **Fantasy feathers:** the shoulder wings from [lesson 10.3](../lessons/module-10/lesson-03.md)
  on the shoulder cap, with single feathers falling down the arm to a feather bracelet.

## You're done when

- [ ] One flow line runs from the shoulder cap along the collarbone and down to the wrist.
- [ ] The big flower sits on the shoulder cap, and the collarbone vine stops before the center line.
- [ ] The arm vine wraps around the arm and does not cross the inside of the elbow.
- [ ] The leaf bracelet closes all the way round the wrist.
- [ ] The design reads from two meters away, stays off straps and the neckline, and only covers skin
      you are comfortable showing.
- [ ] You used only patch-tested face and body paint and removed it gently in the shower.
