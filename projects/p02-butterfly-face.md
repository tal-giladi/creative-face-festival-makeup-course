# Project 2 · Butterfly face

Beginner+ · 60 minutes. The classic full-face butterfly in warm sunset colors: yellow, orange and
magenta upper wings, pink and purple lower wings, a black body down the nose and white details. It
uses everything from the butterfly lesson and looks impressive on every skin tone.

## Finished look

![Project 2 finished on deep skin with the eyes closed: yellow-to-orange-to-magenta upper wings over the brows and eyelids, pink-to-purple lower wings on the cheeks, a black body down the nose bridge, curled antennae, black outlines and veins, white teardrops and dots.](../assets/p02-butterfly-face.svg)

What makes it work: the body is exactly on the center line, both wings are the same size and height,
each wing blends two or three colors, and white teardrops along the edges catch the light.

## Materials

> [!CAUTION]
> The upper wings go over the eyelids. Use only face paint whose label allows use around the eyes,
> paint the lids with the eyes closed, keep paint off the lash line and use no glitter or gems near
> the eyes ([lesson 04.3](../lessons/module-04/lesson-03.md)). If your paint is not labeled
> eye-safe, paint the cheek butterfly from lesson 04.3 instead.

- Two or three sponge pieces (one per color group), a round brush size 2-4, a thin liner.
- Face paint: yellow, orange, magenta or pink, purple, black, white; **eye-safe** for the lids.
- Water, palette, paper towel, fragrance-free wipes, a mirror.
- The `design-3-butterfly` sheet from [labs/module-04](../labs/module-04/).

Substitutes for everything are in [Materials and substitutes](../references/materials.md).

## Step by step

![Butterfly face in five steps, eyes closed. 1: upper wings sponged yellow near the nose, orange, then magenta at the temples. 2: lower wings sponged pink to purple on the cheeks. 3: a black body down the nose bridge and curled antennae. 4: black outer outlines and vein strokes. Done: white teardrops along the edges and white dots.](../assets/p02-butterfly-face-steps.svg)

1. **Plan.** Dot the head between the brows and both upper-wing tips on the forehead, level
   ([lesson 03.4](../lessons/module-03/lesson-04.md)).
2. **Upper wings, eyes closed.** Sponge yellow next to the nose bridge, orange over the brow and lid,
   magenta at the temple; dab where colors meet ([lesson 03.2](../lessons/module-03/lesson-02.md)).
3. **Lower wings.** Eyes open, looking up: pink near the nose to purple at the outer cheek, starting a
   few millimeters below the lower lashes.
4. **Body and antennae.** A black pressure stroke from between the brows down the nose bridge, a
   round head, two thin antennae ending in curls ([lesson 02.3](../lessons/module-02/lesson-03.md)).
5. **Outlines and veins.** Thin black lines on the outer wing edges, two or three veins per wing.
6. **White details.** White teardrops along the wing edges, pointing inward, and white dots. Work
   left wing, then right wing.

## Practice version

Paint it on the `design-3-butterfly` sheet in a sheet protector until both sides match. Then paint
the small cheek butterfly from [lesson 04.3](../lessons/module-04/lesson-03.md) on your hand, and
finally the full face in a mirror.

## Variations

- **Blue morpho:** light blue, blue and purple, like lesson 04.3.
- **Monarch:** orange wings with thick black edges and rows of white dots in the black.
- **Half butterfly:** only one upper and one lower wing on one side, with a vine of dots on the other.

## You're done when

- [ ] The body runs straight down the center line.
- [ ] The wing tips are level and both sides are about the same size.
- [ ] Each wing blends at least two colors with no hard stripes.
- [ ] Outer outlines are thin and clean; white teardrops and dots are on both sides.
- [ ] Only eye-safe paint is on the lids, nothing is on the lash line, and you removed it gently,
  wiping away from the eyes.
