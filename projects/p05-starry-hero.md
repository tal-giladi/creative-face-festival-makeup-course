# Project 5 · Starry hero mask

Beginner+ · 50 minutes. A blue hero mask that fades from light blue to navy, outlined in black, with
yellow stars on the mask, a big star emblem on the forehead, stars on both cheeks and white sparkles.
It combines the mask from lesson 04.6 with the stars from lesson 04.2.

## Finished look

![Project 5 finished on tan skin with open eyes: a blue hero eye mask, light blue at the top to navy at the bottom, outlined in black with a clear gap around each eye, small yellow stars on the mask, a big outlined yellow star on the forehead, outlined stars on both cheeks and white sparkles.](../assets/p05-starry-hero.svg)

What makes it work: the mask is symmetrical with both tips level, the eye holes leave bare skin all
around the eyes, the blue gradient gives depth, and the yellow stars with black outlines and white
shine stand out against the blue.

## Materials

> [!CAUTION]
> The mask goes around the eyes. Use only face paint whose label allows use around the eyes, leave a
> clear gap of bare skin between the paint and the lashes, and put no glitter or gems near the lash
> line ([lesson 04.6](../lessons/module-04/lesson-06.md)). No neon or UV paint near the eyes.

- One or two sponges, a round brush size 2-4, a thin liner.
- Face paint: light blue, blue, navy (or blue plus a little black), yellow, black, white; **eye-safe**
  for the mask.
- Optional: one self-adhesive face gem for the center of the forehead star.
- Water, palette, paper towel, fragrance-free wipes, a mirror.
- The `design-5-hero` sheet from [labs/module-04](../labs/module-04/), or `face-front-open-eyes` from
  [labs/common](../labs/common/).

Substitutes for everything are in [Materials and substitutes](../references/materials.md).

## Step by step

![Starry hero mask in five steps, eyes open. 1: tips and outline sketched with a thin light line, leaving a gap around each eye. 2: sponged light blue at the top to navy at the bottom. 3: a black outline outside and around the eye holes. 4: small yellow stars on the mask, a big star on the forehead and stars on the cheeks. Done: white shine lines, sparkles and dots.](../assets/p05-starry-hero-steps.svg)

1. **Sketch.** Dot both outer tips (level) and the center between the brows; sketch the mask and big
   eye ovals with a thin, light line ([lesson 03.4](../lessons/module-03/lesson-04.md)).
2. **Fill.** Sponge light blue across the top of the mask, blue in the middle and navy along the
   bottom ([lesson 03.2](../lessons/module-03/lesson-02.md)). Eyes closed for the part over the lids,
   looking up for the part under the eyes.
3. **Outline.** Black around the outside and around the eye holes, on the mask edge, not near the
   lashes.
4. **Stars.** Small yellow stars on the mask, one big star on the forehead, one star on each cheek
   ([lesson 04.2](../lessons/module-04/lesson-02.md)). Outline them in black when dry.
5. **Sparkles.** A white shine line along the top of the mask, a white shine in the big star, white
   sparkles and dots around the stars. Optional: one face gem in the middle of the forehead star.

## Practice version

Paint the mask twice on the `design-5-hero` sheet in a sheet protector until both tips are level and
the eye holes are big. Paint a few outlined stars on your hand. Then paint the whole look on your face
in a mirror.

## Variations

- **Red hero:** the red mask from lesson 04.6 with a yellow lightning bolt.
- **Galaxy mask:** purple and navy with tiny white dots like stars and a pink glow at the tips.
- **Royal hero:** add the gold crown from lesson 04.6 above the mask instead of the forehead star.

## You're done when

- [ ] Both outer tips are level and the mask is the same on both sides.
- [ ] Bare skin shows all around each eye; nothing is on the lash line.
- [ ] The blue fades smoothly from light at the top to dark at the bottom.
- [ ] Every star has five even arms, a thin black outline and a white shine on the big one.
- [ ] You removed it gently with an eye-safe remover or warm water, wiping away from the eyes.
