# Project 3 · Flower crown and vine

Beginner+ · 60 minutes. A crown of pink and lilac flowers across the forehead, with a vine that
trails down one temple to the cheek. It looks like a festival flower crown, stays away from the eyes,
and uses only flowers, leaves, vines and dots.

## Finished look

![Project 3 finished on brown skin: a crown of five pink and lilac flowers with leaves across the forehead, the biggest in the middle, and a thin green vine with a small yellow flower and a small pink flower running down one temple to the cheek, with white highlights and dot rows.](../assets/p03-flower-crown.svg)

What makes it work: the flowers follow a gentle arc above the brows with the biggest in the middle,
sizes step down toward the sides, leaves fill the gaps, and the vine leads the eye from the crown down
to the cheek.

## Materials

> [!WARNING]
> Use only face paint you have patch-tested ([lesson 01.3](../lessons/module-01/lesson-03.md)).
> Keep the crown above the brows and the vine on the temple and cheek, outside the eye area. Tie hair
> back so it doesn't drag through wet paint.

- Round brush size 2-4, a thin liner, one sponge.
- Face paint: pink, lilac or purple, yellow, orange, green, light green, black, white.
- Water, palette, paper towel, fragrance-free wipes, a mirror, a hairband.
- The `design-1-flowers` sheet from [labs/module-04](../labs/module-04/).

Substitutes for everything are in [Materials and substitutes](../references/materials.md).

## Step by step

![Flower crown and vine in five steps. 1: a soft pink glow sponged across the forehead and a lilac glow down one temple. 2: five flowers in an arc above the brows and two small flowers on the temple and cheek. 3: yellow centers, a thin green vine and leaves between the flowers. 4: thin black outlines on the flowers. Done: white curves on the petals and rows of white dots.](../assets/p03-flower-crown-steps.svg)

1. **Glow.** Sponge a pale pink band across the forehead and a little lilac down one temple
   ([lesson 03.2](../lessons/module-03/lesson-02.md)).
2. **Flowers.** The biggest flower in the middle of the forehead, then one each side a little lower
   and smaller, then one more each side. Two small flowers on the temple and cheek
   ([lesson 04.1](../lessons/module-04/lesson-01.md)).
3. **Leaves and vine.** Yellow centers. A thin green vine from the crown down the temple to the cheek,
   ending in a curl. Leaves tucked between the flowers and along the vine.
4. **Outline.** A thin black outline on the flowers, once they are dry
   ([lesson 03.3](../lessons/module-03/lesson-03.md)).
5. **Highlights.** A white curve on each petal, white dots on the centers, a row of white dots under
   the crown and a short dot trail on the cheek ([lesson 02.4](../lessons/module-02/lesson-04.md)).

## Practice version

Paint the crown and vine on the left side of the `design-1-flowers` sheet in a sheet protector. Then
paint two flowers and a short vine on your forearm, then the whole design on your forehead in a mirror.

## Variations

- **Sunflower crown:** yellow and orange petals with brown centers on dark green leaves.
- **Night garden:** white and light blue flowers on a soft purple glow.
- **Half crown:** flowers only on one side of the forehead, flowing into the vine.

## You're done when

- [ ] The flowers follow an even arc above the brows, biggest in the middle.
- [ ] Flower sizes step down toward the sides and are similar on left and right.
- [ ] Leaves are tucked between flowers; the vine ends in a curl.
- [ ] Outlines are thin, and each flower has white highlights.
- [ ] Nothing is in the eye area; you took a photo and washed it off with soap and water.
