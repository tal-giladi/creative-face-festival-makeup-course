# Project 12 · Your original face design

Advanced · 180 minutes. The final project of the core course: a complete face design that is yours, from the first word of the brief to a finished portfolio page. The example below, a one-sided Sunset phoenix, shows the process and the level to aim for; your design will be different, and that is the point. Spread the work over two or three sessions if you like.

## Finished look

![Project 12 example on brown skin: Sunset phoenix, an original one-sided design. A flame swirl in red, orange and yellow runs from the right temple down the side of the face and curls on the cheekbone, below the eye area. Two small feathers on the left cheekbone echo the design. Gold filigree curls, yellow and orange dot trails, white sparkles, a red teardrop gem at the base of the feathers and small yellow and orange gems finish it.](../assets/p12-original-design.svg)

![The process behind the Sunset phoenix. Brief: Sunset phoenix, a summer party lasting 4 hours, one side, warm colors, no paint near the eyes; a warm palette of yellow, orange, red, magenta and gold, with a feather and a sunset sky as references. Thumbnails: feathers on both sides, feathers on one side, and a fan rising from the chin; the one-sided version is chosen because it reads best and is new. What makes the example work: a brief with clear constraints (one side, warm colors, eye area clear);
references used for single ideas (the feather's shape, the sunset's colors) and mixed into something
new; a big star zone (the feather fan) with a smaller echo on the other side; feathers that use the
three-color blend and one-stroke ideas of Module 8; and a white underlayer so warm colors stay bright
on brown skin.

## Materials

> [!CAUTION]
> Whatever you design, the rules stay: only products sold for skin and patch-tested
> ([lesson 01.3](../lessons/module-01/lesson-03.md)); in the eye area only eye-safe products, never
> on the lash line or waterline ([lesson 05.4](../lessons/module-05/lesson-04.md)); **no glitter,
> gems or neon paint near the eyes**; skin-safe adhesives only for gems
> ([lesson 06.3](../lessons/module-06/lesson-03.md)). Never paint over irritated or broken skin.
> Choose an invented theme, not a sacred or cultural design worn as a costume
> ([lesson 09.3](../lessons/module-09/lesson-03.md)).

- Everything your design needs from Modules 1-9: face paint in your palette plus black and white,
  sponges, round, liner and flat brushes, and cosmetic glitter or face gems if you use them.
- The `mood-palette`, `idea-thumbnails` and `portfolio-page` sheets from
  [labs/module-09](../labs/module-09/), and `face-map` and `face-front` from
  [labs/common](../labs/common/).
- The [Design plan](../templates/design-plan.md) and [Self-check](../templates/self-check.md)
  templates.
- A phone camera, window light and a plain background.

Substitutes for everything are in [Materials and substitutes](../references/materials.md).

## Step by step

The example, painted in five steps:

![Project 12, the Sunset phoenix, in five pictures on brown skin. 1: a light sketch of the six feather lines fanning from above the right brow and the swirl line down the side of the face, with the eye area kept clear. 2: a soft sponge glow of yellow, orange and magenta behind the feathers. 3: a thin white layer under each feather, then each feather in yellow, orange, red and magenta from base to tip, with a pale shaft. 4: the flame swirl down the side of the face with a black outline, and two small feathers on the left cheekbone. Done: gold filigree curls, dot trails, sparkles and gems at the base of the feathers.](../assets/p12-original-design-steps.svg)

For your own design, follow the loop of [lesson 09.4](../lessons/module-09/lesson-04.md):

1. **Brief and constraints.** Two or three lines: what, where, how long. Choose at least one
   constraint (one side, three colors, a time limit).
2. **References.** Three or four pictures; write the one idea you take from each on the
   `mood-palette` planner.
3. **Features, shapes and color story.** 3-5 features as simple shapes
   ([lesson 09.1](../lessons/module-09/lesson-01.md)), a palette from [lesson 03.5](../lessons/module-03/lesson-05.md) and three mood words.
4. **Thumbnails.** At least three on the `idea-thumbnails` sheet; distance test; choose one.
5. **Plan.** Place it on the `face-map`; fill in the Design plan with parts, order of work and the
   products check. Mark the eye area.
6. **Versions on the template.** Paint version 1 on a `face-front` sheet, self-check, choose one
   goal, paint version 2 (and 3 if needed).
7. **Paint it on your face** in a mirror, following your order of work: base and blends, main
   shapes, linework, details, eye-safe makeup, then glitter and gems last.
8. **Photograph** it: front from one meter, a close-up, window light, plain background.
9. **Portfolio page.** Fill in the `portfolio-page`: photos, brief, palette, references, what
   worked, next time, and the self-check.

## Practice version

Paint every version on the `face-front` sheet in a sheet protector before you touch your face, and
practice the hardest part (a texture, a blend or a filigree) on paper and on your forearm first. For
the phoenix example: one feather on paper five times until the blend from yellow to magenta is
smooth, then on the forearm.

## Variations

- **Same design, new palette:** paint your finished design again with a different color story and
  compare the two portfolio pages.
- **Symmetrical version:** mirror your one-sided design (or the other way round) and see which reads
  better.
- **Character version:** turn your design into a character with horns, wings or leaves from
  [lesson 09.3](../lessons/module-09/lesson-03.md).
- **Mask version:** build your design into a masquerade mask shape from [lesson 09.2](../lessons/module-09/lesson-02.md).

## You're done when

- [ ] Your brief, constraints and references (one idea each) are written down.
- [ ] You drew at least three thumbnails and painted at least two versions on the template.
- [ ] The final design reads in one second from three meters and has one clear star zone.
- [ ] The eye area has only eye-safe products; glitter and gems are outside it.
- [ ] Your self-check is done and your portfolio page has photos, notes and one goal for next time.
