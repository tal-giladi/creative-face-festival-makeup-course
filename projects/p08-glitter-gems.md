# Project 8 · Glitter and gems

Intermediate · 50 minutes. A sparkling festival face with no paint at all: gold cosmetic glitter
along the cheekbones, a graded gem line arching above each brow to the temple, a jewel in the center
of the forehead, and glitter roots along the hair parting. It combines lessons 06.2 and 06.3.

## Finished look

![Project 8 finished on fair skin, eyes open: gold cosmetic glitter in a crescent along each cheekbone up to the temple with bigger pink and white flakes on the edge, a pink drop gem in the center of the forehead with three small clear gems, a line of pink and clear gems arching above each brow and ending in a pink drop gem at the temple, gold glitter along the hair parting, and white sparkles. Nothing on the eyelids.](../assets/p08-glitter-gems.svg)

What makes it work: the glitter crescents and the gem arches both curve **around** the eye area, so
together they frame the eyes like a mask without touching the lids. The gems are graded (big at the
temple and forehead, small toward the nose), and the forehead jewel gives the face a center point.

## Materials

> [!CAUTION]
> No glitter or gems on the eyelids, the lash line or under the eyes: flakes and gems can fall into
> the eye and scratch it (American Academy of Ophthalmology). Only cosmetic glitter and gems or glue
> sold for skin, patch-tested; **never craft glitter, superglue, nail glue or craft glue**. Latex-free
> lash glue if you use loose gems. Keep gems and glitter away from small children
> ([lesson 06.3](../lessons/module-06/lesson-03.md)).

- Cosmetic glitter in gold (or bio glitter), plus a few bigger flakes in pink and white.
- A glitter base: cosmetic glitter gel, or plain fragrance-free aloe vera gel.
- Self-adhesive face gems: two pink drop gems for the temples, one bigger drop for the forehead, and
  about 20 small round gems in pink and clear. Or loose flat-backed gems and latex-free lash glue.
- A small flat brush, a clean fluffy brush, tweezers, an eye-safe pencil for planning dots.
- Sticky tape, an oil cleanser and cotton pads for removal.
- The `design-p08-glitter-gems`, `glitter-map` and `gem-map` sheets from [labs/module-06](../labs/module-06/).

Substitutes for everything are in [Materials and substitutes](../references/materials.md).

## Step by step

![Glitter and gems in five steps on fair skin. 1: a thin crescent of glitter base on each cheekbone up to the temple, and a thin line along the hair parting. 2: gold glitter patted onto the base and the loose flakes tapped off. 3: the biggest gems, a pink drop in the center of the forehead and one at each temple. 4: a line of smaller gems arching above each brow, getting smaller toward the nose. Done: tiny clear gems, bigger flakes on the glitter edge and sparkles.](../assets/p08-glitter-gems-steps.svg)

1. **Glitter base.** On clean, dry skin, paint a thin crescent of base from each temple down along the
   top of the cheekbone, curving around the outside of the eye area. Add a thin line of gel along the
   hair parting ([lesson 06.2](../lessons/module-06/lesson-02.md)).
2. **Glitter.** Pat gold glitter onto the base, then close your eyes and tap off the loose flakes with
   a clean fluffy brush, outward toward the ears.
3. **Big gems.** Dot the gem lines with an eye-safe pencil first. Then place the forehead drop on the
   center line and one drop gem at each temple, level on both sides
   ([lesson 06.3](../lessons/module-06/lesson-03.md)).
4. **Gem lines.** From each temple gem, place gems along the dots above the brow, along the brow
   bone, each a little smaller toward the nose. Check in your phone camera that both arches match.
5. **Details.** Three tiny clear gems around the forehead drop, a few bigger pink and white flakes
   pressed along the outer edge of the glitter, and nothing below the brows.
6. **Removal later:** gems first (oil on the edge, lift), then glitter (tape, oil, wipe away from the
   eyes, wash).

## Practice version

On the `design-p08-glitter-gems` sheet in a sheet protector, stick self-adhesive gems on the plastic
along the gray dots and fill the crescents with a pencil or face paint. Check that both sides match.
Then glitter a crescent and stick a short gem line on your forearm, and take them off again with tape
and oil. Then do the look on your face in a mirror.

## Variations

- **Silver moon:** silver glitter, clear and pale blue gems, and a crescent-moon gem cluster on the
  forehead.
- **Rainbow roots:** holographic glitter along the parting and the hairline, and gems in rainbow order
  above the brows.
- **Paint plus sparkle:** paint a bold color crescent first ([lesson 05.1](../lessons/module-05/lesson-01.md)),
  then glitter its edge while the paint is still slightly damp, and add the gem arches.

## You're done when

- [ ] The glitter crescents curve around the eye area and keep crisp edges.
- [ ] The gem arches sit above the brows, are graded in size and match on both sides.
- [ ] The forehead jewel sits on the center line.
- [ ] Nothing is on the eyelids, the lash line or under the eyes.
- [ ] Every product was sold for skin and patch-tested, and you removed everything gently: gems with
      oil from the edge, glitter with tape and oil, wiping away from the eyes.
