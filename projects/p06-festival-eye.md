# Project 6 · Festival eye

Intermediate · 60 minutes. A bold, symmetrical festival eye: a sharp black wing, a cyan floating
crease, a magenta-to-violet glow on the brow bone and dots under the brows, framed outside the eye
area by chevrons and dot trails at the temples and a gem at each tip. It combines the eye designs
from [lesson 05.4](../lessons/module-05/lesson-04.md) with the dots and geometric lines of Module 7, and keeps every product near the eyes
eye-safe.

## Finished look

![Project 6 finished on medium skin: a bold festival eye on both eyes. Eye-safe black liner with a long, sharp wing; a cyan floating crease line above the natural crease; a soft magenta to violet blend on the brow bone; white and gold dots under each brow. Outside the eye area: three nested chevrons in magenta, purple and cyan above each brow tail pointing up and out toward the temple, with a violet drop gem at the tip; magenta dots above the brow; a cyan dot trail from the temple down to the cheekbone ending in a small blue gem.](../assets/p06-festival-eye.svg)

What makes it work: both wings point to the same height, the floating crease shows with the eyes
open, the brow-bone glow lifts the eyes, and the temple chevrons carry the design up and out. The
split is the safety rule made visible: soft color and liner inside the eye area are eye-safe
products; paint, gems and anything neon stay outside it.

## Materials

> [!CAUTION]
> Inside the eye area (brows down to the top of the cheekbones) use **only products whose label
> allows eye-area use**: eye-safe liner, eye-safe face paint or your own eye shadow. Nothing on the
> waterline. **No neon, UV or glow-in-the-dark paint, no glitter and no gems near the eyes**: the
> chevrons, dot trails and gems sit outside the eye area only
> ([lesson 05.4](../lessons/module-05/lesson-04.md), [lesson 06.4](../lessons/module-06/lesson-04.md),
> [lesson 06.3](../lessons/module-06/lesson-03.md)). Never share eye makeup. Contact lenses go in
> before you start.

- Eye-safe black liner (liquid, gel or a sharp pencil) and your own mascara.
- Eye-safe color for the crease and brow bone: face paint labeled for the eye area, or powder eye
  shadow in cyan, magenta and violet.
- Face paint for the temples: magenta, purple, cyan, white, black (UV versions only if labeled for
  skin, and only at the temples and cheekbones).
- A liner brush size 0-1, a small angled or round brush, a dotting tool, a small clean sponge.
- Two self-adhesive face gems per side, or flat-backed gems with latex-free lash glue (Module 6).
- Cotton buds and an eye-safe remover for fixes; a mirror at eye height.
- The `festival-eye` sheet from [labs/module-07](../labs/module-07/), or `eye-area` from
  [labs/common](../labs/common/).

Substitutes for everything are in [Materials and substitutes](../references/materials.md).

## Step by step

![Project 6 in six pictures, eyes open, on medium skin. 1: the eye area in mind, the wing angle from the lower lash line toward the brow tail, and dots for the wing tip and the temple point. 2: eye-safe liner along the upper lash line, pulled out to the wing tip. 3: a cyan floating crease line just above the natural crease. 4: a magenta to violet blend of eye-safe color on the brow bone, and white and gold dots under the brows. 5: outside the eye area, three nested chevrons above each brow tail and dot trails down the temple. Done: mascara, a drop gem at each chevron tip and a small gem at the end of each dot trail.](../assets/p06-festival-eye-steps.svg)

1. **Prep and plan.** Clean, lightly moisturized, dry skin ([lesson 05.2](../lessons/module-05/lesson-02.md)).
   Check every label. Look straight ahead and imagine the lower lash line continuing toward the end
   of the brow: dot the wing tip there, same height on both eyes. Dot the start of each temple
   chevron above the brow tail.
2. **Liner and wing.** Eye-safe liner along the upper lash line, outside the lashes, thin at the
   inner corner and thicker toward the outside, out to the dot. Then the other eye.
3. **Floating crease.** With the eyes open, a thin cyan line just above the natural crease, from the
   middle of the lid out toward the wing. Both eyes.
4. **Brow bone and dots.** Dab magenta at the outer brow bone and violet toward the middle with a
   small sponge or brush, eye-safe products only. Then white and gold dots along the curve under
   each brow ([lesson 07.1](../lessons/module-07/lesson-01.md)).
5. **Temple chevrons and dot trails.** Outside the eye area, three nested chevrons above each brow
   tail, pointing up and out ([lesson 07.2](../lessons/module-07/lesson-02.md)), black outline then
   color. A cyan dot trail from the temple down toward the cheekbone, big to small, from one dip.
   Magenta dots above the brow.
6. **Finish.** Mascara. Close your eyes and set the painted temple parts. Then one drop gem at each
   chevron tip and a small gem at the end of each dot trail. Check with your eyes open and closed.

## Practice version

On the `festival-eye` sheet in a sheet protector, trace the gray design on both eyes, then paint it
yourself from the gray start dots in the lower half. Practice the wing ten times on the back of your
hand. Then paint the whole look on your face in a mirror.

## Variations

- **Sunset eye:** orange and magenta brow bone, a yellow floating crease, gold dots, orange chevrons.
- **UV night eye:** the same eye design with ordinary eye-safe colors, and UV face paint only on the
  temple chevrons and dot trails, which glow under black light while the eye area stays dark.
- **Mandala temple:** replace the chevrons with a small half mandala of dots at each temple.

## You're done when

- [ ] Every product inside the eye area is labeled for eye-area use; nothing is on the waterline.
- [ ] Both wings point to the same height, and the floating crease shows with the eyes open.
- [ ] Chevrons, dot trails and gems sit outside the eye area, mirrored on both sides.
- [ ] No neon, UV, glitter or gems are near the eyes.
- [ ] You removed it gently: gems lifted from the edge, eye-safe remover around the eyes, wiping away
      from them.
