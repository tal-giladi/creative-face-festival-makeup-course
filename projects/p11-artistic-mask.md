# Project 11 · Artistic mask

Advanced · 120 minutes. A royal masquerade mask in plum and gold: shaded so it looks lifted off the face, edged with white lace, covered in fine gold filigree, with painted feathers sweeping up from one temple and jewels at the tips. It brings together lesson 09.2 with the 3D shading and lace of Module 8.

## Finished look

![Project 11 finished on light skin: a royal masquerade mask. A plum mask, light at the top and almost black at the bottom, covers the brows and the upper cheeks and rises to a point at each temple, leaving a wide almond of bare skin around each open eye. It has 3D shading: dark along every edge, light bands over the brows and on the cheek curve, and a soft shadow on the skin. A white lace edge of scallops and dots runs along the lower edge. Gold filigree vines curl along the brow band, the temple tips and the cheek band. Three painted feathers in plum and violet with gold shafts rise from the left temple over the forehead. A red teardrop gem sits above the center, blue gems at the temple tips and two small pearls on each side.](../assets/p11-artistic-mask.svg)

What makes it work: one elegant symmetrical shape; shading that turns a flat color into something
solid; lace and filigree thin enough to look delicate; one asymmetric touch (the feathers) that
makes it look designed rather than stamped; and bare skin all around the eyes.

## Materials

> [!CAUTION]
> The whole mask surrounds the eyes: every paint in it must be labeled **eye-safe**, and a wide
> almond of bare skin stays around each eye. Nothing on the lash line or waterline. **No glitter,
> gems or neon paint near the eyes**; gems go on the forehead and temple tips only
> ([lesson 06.3](../lessons/module-06/lesson-03.md)). Patch-test everything
> ([lesson 01.3](../lessons/module-01/lesson-03.md)) and remove it gently with your eyes closed,
> wiping away from the eyes.

- Eye-safe face paint: plum or purple, a dark purple (or purple with a little black), white, black;
  metallic gold labeled for the eye area, or yellow with a touch of orange.
- Violet and lilac face paint for the feathers (these sit on the forehead, outside the eye area).
- Sponges: two small wedges.
- Brushes: liner size 0-1, round size 3-4.
- Three self-adhesive face gems and two small pearl gems (optional; or painted jewels).
- The `artistic-mask` and `filigree-drill` sheets from [labs/module-09](../labs/module-09/).

Substitutes for everything are in [Materials and substitutes](../references/materials.md).

## Step by step

![Project 11, the royal masquerade mask, in six pictures on light skin. 1: a light sketch: center and temple dots, the outline and a wide almond around each eye. 2: eye-safe plum sponged inside the outline, lighter at the top. 3: 3D shading along the edges, light bands on the brow and cheek curve, a soft shadow below. 4: three painted feathers rising from the left temple, a black outline and the white lace edge. 5: gold filigree vines, curls, teardrops and dots. Done: white sparkles, a teardrop gem above the center, gems at the temple tips and small pearls, all far from the eyes.](../assets/p11-artistic-mask-steps.svg)

1. **Warm up** on the `filigree-drill` sheet: C-scrolls, S-scrolls and the lace row
   ([lesson 09.2](../lessons/module-09/lesson-02.md)).
2. **Sketch.** Dot the center between the brows and both temple tips at the same height. Sketch the
   outline and a wide almond around each eye.
3. **Base.** Sponge plum inside the outline, lighter at the top, darker toward the cheeks. Eyes
   closed for the brow band; looking up for the cheek band.
4. **3D shading.** Dark purple just inside every edge, softened inward; a lighter tint over each brow
   and along the cheek curve; a very soft shadow line below the lower edge
   ([lesson 08.2](../lessons/module-08/lesson-02.md)). Let it dry.
5. **Feathers.** From the left temple tip, three long leaf shapes sweeping up over the forehead:
   violet at the base, lilac at the tip, a fine gold shaft and a few pale barb lines.
6. **Outline and lace.** A thin black outline around the mask and the almonds. Along the lower edge:
   scallops, a dot in each, tiny dots outside ([lesson 08.3](../lessons/module-08/lesson-03.md)).
7. **Filigree.** On one side: a vine along the brow band ending in a curl, two small curls branching
   up, a curl rising to the temple tip, and a vine along the cheek band. Mirror it, then add
   teardrops and dots.
8. **Jewels last.** White sparkles on the light bands; a teardrop gem above the center, a gem at each
   temple tip, two pearls along the top edge.

## Practice version

Paint the mask on the `artistic-mask` sheet in a sheet protector: first base and shading only, wipe,
then the full mask with lace and filigree. Paint one filigree vine and a feather on the back of your
hand before you start on your face.

## Variations

- **Black and gold:** a black-to-charcoal mask with gold filigree and white lace; striking on every
  skin tone.
- **Ocean mask:** teal to navy with silver filigree and pearl gems; the scales of lesson 09.3 on the
  temple tips.
- **Half mask:** only the right side, sweeping up into big feathers; the left side bare with a small
  filigree echo above the brow.
- **Different shape:** the cat-eye, butterfly or swept-up shapes from lesson 09.2.

## You're done when

- [ ] Every paint near the eyes is eye-safe, and a clear almond of bare skin surrounds each eye.
- [ ] Both temple tips are at the same height and the shape is symmetrical.
- [ ] The mask looks raised: darker edges, light bands and a soft shadow.
- [ ] Lace and filigree are thin, clean and mirrored; curls end in points, not blobs.
- [ ] Gems sit only on the forehead and temple tips; you photographed the look and removed it gently.
