# Project 4 · Tiger face

Beginner+ · 50 minutes. A full tiger: an orange and yellow sponged base, white fur over the brows,
a white muzzle and chin, a black nose and tapered black stripes mirrored on both sides. It is the
most requested design at face-painting stands and a great test of your pressure strokes.

## Finished look

![Project 4 finished on light skin: an orange face with a yellow center, white over the brows with a furry top edge, a white muzzle and chin, a black nose and mouth line, tapered black stripes on the forehead, temples, cheeks and jaw, mirrored on both sides, and whisker dots.](../assets/p04-tiger-face.svg)

What makes it work: the base blends from orange at the edges to yellow in the middle, the stripes
start wide at the edge of the face and taper to a point, they are of different lengths, and they
mirror each other left and right.

## Materials

> [!WARNING]
> Use only face paint you have patch-tested ([lesson 01.3](../lessons/module-01/lesson-03.md)).
> Sponge around the eyes, not on the eyelids or the lash line, and keep paint out of the nostrils
> and off the inside of the lips.

- Two or three sponges, a round brush size 3-4 with a good point, a thin liner.
- Face paint: orange (or red plus yellow), yellow, white, black.
- Water, palette, paper towel, fragrance-free wipes, a mirror, a hairband.
- The `design-4-tiger` sheet from [labs/module-04](../labs/module-04/).

Substitutes for everything are in [Materials and substitutes](../references/materials.md).

## Step by step

![Tiger face in five steps. 1: orange sponged over the face and yellow in the middle, clear of the eyes. 2: white over the brows, on the muzzle and on the chin. 3: a black nose and a line to the lip. 4: tapered black stripes from the edges inward, mirrored. Done: whisker dots, a shine on the nose and mouth corners.](../assets/p04-tiger-face-steps.svg)

1. **Base.** Sponge orange over the face, around the eyes; dab yellow in the middle. Let it dry
   ([lesson 03.2](../lessons/module-03/lesson-02.md)).
2. **White areas.** White over each brow with a flicked, furry top edge; a white muzzle on the upper
   lip; a white chin patch.
3. **Nose.** Black on the tip of the nose, a line down to the lip, short lines at the mouth corners.
4. **Stripes.** Forehead center first, then the sides, temples, cheeks and jaw. Press at the edge of
   the face, pull inward and lift to a point. Left, then the same stripe right
   ([lesson 04.5](../lessons/module-04/lesson-05.md)).
5. **Details.** Whisker dots on the muzzle and a white shine dot on the nose
   ([lesson 03.3](../lessons/module-03/lesson-03.md)).

## Practice version

Sponge a sheet of paper orange and paint 20 stripes on it first. Then paint the whole tiger on the
`design-4-tiger` sheet in a sheet protector. Then a few stripes and a nose on your forearm, then the
full face in a mirror.

## Variations

- **White tiger:** white and light gray base, light blue on the brow bone, black stripes, pink nose.
- **Rainbow tiger:** sponge the base in rainbow bands across the face, then the same white areas and
  stripes.
- **Half tiger:** tiger on one half of the face, your own skin or a flower vine on the other half.

## You're done when

- [ ] The base blends from orange at the edges to yellow in the middle, with no streaks.
- [ ] White over the brows, muzzle and chin, with no paint on the eyelids.
- [ ] Every stripe tapers from wide at the edge to a sharp point.
- [ ] The stripes mirror each other and are of different lengths.
- [ ] You took a photo and washed it off with warm water and soap.
