# Project 9 · Symmetrical trance face

Intermediate · 120 minutes. A full-face, symmetrical trance look: a dot mandala on the third eye,
rainbow swirls that rise from between the brows and curl at the temples, nested chevrons and dot
lines on the cheekbones, a small accent on the chin, and gems placed last. It brings together every
technique of Module 7 and the festival rules of Modules 5 and 6.

## Finished look

![Project 9 finished on fair skin: a symmetrical trance face. On the forehead center, a small dot mandala in magenta, cyan, purple and yellow with a pink gem in the middle. From between the brows, two swirls of yellow, orange, magenta and purple bands outlined in black rise over the brows and curl at the temples, with a blue gem in each curl, white highlight lines and white dots along the outside. On each cheekbone, three nested chevrons in magenta, orange and yellow point up and out, with a cyan dot line below them. A purple triangle with dots on the chin. Eye-safe liner and mascara; nothing on the eyelids.](../assets/p09-symmetrical-trance.svg)

What makes it work: one star zone (the swirls) with the mandala and chevrons as accents; every part
mirrored at the same landmarks; a warm rainbow palette with cyan as the cool accent; clean black
lines that keep it readable from the dance floor; and gems only outside the eye area.

## Materials

> [!CAUTION]
> Use only patch-tested products sold for skin ([lesson 01.3](../lessons/module-01/lesson-03.md)).
> Only eye-safe liner and mascara near the eyes, nothing on the waterline
> ([lesson 05.4](../lessons/module-05/lesson-04.md)). **UV/neon paint never near the eyes**
> ([lesson 06.4](../lessons/module-06/lesson-04.md)). Cosmetic glitter and gems only, never on the
> eyelids or near the lash line ([lesson 06.2](../lessons/module-06/lesson-02.md),
> [lesson 06.3](../lessons/module-06/lesson-03.md)). Keep gems away from small children.

- Face paint: yellow, orange, magenta, purple, cyan, black, white (your palette, or UV versions
  labeled for skin for the forehead and cheekbone parts).
- Brushes: round size 3-4, liner size 0-1; a dotting tool or brush handle; a sponge.
- Skin prep and setting: gentle cleanser, light moisturizer, setting spray or translucent powder
  ([lesson 05.2](../lessons/module-05/lesson-02.md)).
- Your own eye-safe liner and mascara.
- Four self-adhesive face gems (or flat-backed gems and latex-free lash glue); optional cosmetic
  glitter gel.
- The `trance-face` sheet and the `festival-look-plan` sheet from
  [labs/module-07](../labs/module-07/), and the [Design plan](../templates/design-plan.md).

Substitutes for everything are in [Materials and substitutes](../references/materials.md).

## Step by step

![Project 9 in seven pictures on fair skin. 1: prep, then a light sketch: the forehead center mark and circle, a swirl lead line on each side, three points on each cheekbone. 2: the swirl bands, yellow, orange, magenta and purple, both sides. 3: the forehead mandala: center, ring and petals. 4: black linework on all the band edges, and a magenta dot with yellow dots between the brows. 5: nested chevrons and cyan dot lines on the cheekbones. 6: white highlights and dots, mandala trails and the chin triangle. Done: eye-safe liner and mascara, set the face, then gems on the mandala, in each curl and between the brows, all outside the eye area.](../assets/p09-symmetrical-trance-steps.svg)

1. **Plan** on the `festival-look-plan` sheet: palette, star zone (swirls), accents, order and
   minutes ([lesson 07.4](../lessons/module-07/lesson-04.md)).
2. **Prep and sketch.** Prep the skin. Mark the forehead center, sketch the swirl lead line on one
   side and then the other, and dot three points on each cheekbone. Check from a distance.
3. **Swirl bands.** Lightest band beside the lead line, on the outside of the curl, then the next
   colors in rainbow order, both sides band by band ([lesson 07.3](../lessons/module-07/lesson-03.md)).
   Let them dry.
4. **Mandala.** Center dot, a ring by quarters, petals, an outer ring
   ([lesson 07.1](../lessons/module-07/lesson-01.md)).
5. **Linework.** Black lines over every band edge, thicker on the outside. A magenta dot with yellow
   dots between the brows where the swirls meet.
6. **Cheekbones.** Three nested chevrons on each side, pointing up and out, with a cyan dot line
   below ([lesson 07.2](../lessons/module-07/lesson-02.md)).
7. **White details.** Highlight lines in the yellow bands, dots along the outside of the swirls,
   mandala trails, and the chin triangle.
8. **Eyes and set.** Eye-safe liner and mascara, then set the face with your eyes and mouth closed.
9. **Gems last.** One on the mandala center, one in each curl, one between the brows. Optional: a
   little cosmetic glitter gel on the upper cheekbones, above the chevrons and well away from the
   eyes.
10. **Photos** in daylight close up and from three meters, and under UV light if you used UV paint.

## Practice version

Trace the design on the `trance-face` sheet in a sheet protector, then paint it again in your own
palette. Practice the swirl bands on the `wave-drill` sheet and the mandala on the
`dot-mandala-grids` sheet. Then paint the full look on your face in a mirror, with a timer.

## Variations

- **Cool trance:** lime, teal, blue and purple bands with a magenta accent.
- **UV trance:** the same design with UV face paint on the swirls, mandala and chevrons only; the eye
  area keeps ordinary eye-safe makeup and stays dark under black light.
- **Geometric trance:** replace the swirls with mirrored triangles and a diamond on the third eye
  from lesson 07.2.
- **Glitter trance:** cosmetic glitter on the outer swirl band at the temples (Module 6 rules).

## You're done when

- [ ] A design plan with palette, star zone, order and timing was made before painting.
- [ ] The swirls start on the center line and curl at the same height on both temples; bands keep an
      even width with clean black lines.
- [ ] The mandala is centered and even; the chevrons and dot lines mirror each other.
- [ ] Only eye-safe makeup is in the eye area; UV paint, glitter and gems are outside it.
- [ ] The look reads from three meters away, survived a rub test on its edge, and came off gently at
      night.
