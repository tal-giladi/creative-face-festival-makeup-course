# Project 10 · Fantasy character

Advanced · 120 minutes. An emerald dragon: shimmering scales that grow out of the skin from the forehead around the temples and down the cheeks, gold horns with real 3D shading, a crest of spikes down the center and a sharp eye-safe liner flick. It uses the character method of [lesson 09.3](../lessons/module-09/lesson-03.md) and the blends, 3D shapes and scales of Module 8.

## Finished look

![Project 10 finished on medium skin: an emerald dragon. Green-to-teal scales, fading into the skin, run from the forehead above each brow around the temple and down the outer cheek to the jaw, edged on the inside with a thin dark line. Two gold horns with ridges and a soft shadow rise from the upper forehead and curve outward. A column of four gold spikes runs down the center of the forehead to an orange teardrop gem between the brows. Eye-safe black liner ends in a sharp flick toward each temple. White and gold dots and two sparkles finish it.](../assets/p10-fantasy-character.svg)

What makes it work: three key features (scales, horns, crest) and one color story (emerald, teal,
gold, black) that reads as "dragon" in one second; the scales fade into the skin instead of ending
in a hard edge; the horns look solid because one side is dark and the other light; and the eye area
holds nothing but eye-safe liner.

## Materials

> [!CAUTION]
> Only patch-tested products sold for skin ([lesson 01.3](../lessons/module-01/lesson-03.md)). The
> liner flick and anything else near the eyes must be **eye-safe**; nothing on the lash line or
> waterline ([lesson 05.4](../lessons/module-05/lesson-04.md)). No glitter, gems or neon paint near
> the eyes. Hold a scale stencil or fishnet on the temple and cheek only, never on the eyelid.

- Face paint: lime green, emerald, deep teal, gold (or yellow plus orange), dark brown, black, white.
- A split cake in green tones (optional, [lesson 08.1](../lessons/module-08/lesson-01.md)).
- Sponges: two or three, cut into wedges.
- Brushes: round size 3-4, liner size 0-1, flat 1-1.5 cm.
- A scale stencil or new, washed fishnet fabric (optional, [lesson 08.3](../lessons/module-08/lesson-03.md)).
- Your own eye-safe black liner.
- One self-adhesive face gem (optional; or a painted gem).
- The `fantasy-character` and `mood-palette` sheets from [labs/module-09](../labs/module-09/).

Substitutes for everything are in [Materials and substitutes](../references/materials.md).

## Step by step

![Project 10, the emerald dragon, in six pictures on medium skin. 1: a light sketch: the scale area from the forehead around the temple to the jaw, the horn line and the center line, with the eye area kept clear. 2: a sponge blend of lime, emerald and deep teal over the scale area, darker toward the jaw. 3: scales, row by row, with a stencil or freehand, fading into the skin at the edges. 4: gold horns shaded dark on one side and light on the other, with ridges, and a column of gold spikes down the center. 5: a dark line along the inner edge of the scales and eye-safe liner with a sharp flick. Done: white and gold highlight dots, sparkles and a gem between the brows.](../assets/p10-fantasy-character-steps.svg)

1. **Plan** on the `mood-palette` planner: features (scales, horns, crest), color story, mood words
   ([lesson 09.3](../lessons/module-09/lesson-03.md)).
2. **Sketch** lightly: the scale band from above the brow around the temple to the jaw on one side,
   then the other; the horn lines from the upper forehead curving out; the center line. Keep the
   eye area empty.
3. **Sponge blend.** Lime at the top of the band, emerald in the middle, deep teal toward the jaw
   ([lesson 08.1](../lessons/module-08/lesson-01.md)). Fade the inner edge softly. Let it dry.
4. **Scales.** With the stencil, dab a darker teal through it, then lift it straight off. Freehand:
   dot the start of each row, paint U shapes from the bottom row up, then a thin light curve inside
   each scale. Fewer and softer scales at the edges.
5. **Horns.** Gold or yellow fill, a dark brown side, a pale highlight line on the other side, then
   curved ridge lines across, the 3D method of [lesson 08.2](../lessons/module-08/lesson-02.md).
   Outline in black.
6. **Crest.** Four gold spikes down the center of the forehead, biggest at the top, outlined in black.
7. **Lines and liner.** A thin dark teal line along the inner edge of the scales. Eye-safe black
   liner along the upper lashes ending in a sharp flick toward the temple.
8. **Finish.** White dots on the scales near the brow, gold dots on the cheek, a sparkle on each
   horn, and a gem between the brows (above the eye area).

## Practice version

Trace the scale area, horns and crest on the `fantasy-character` sheet in a sheet protector, and
paint the whole dragon once on the plastic. Practice a 6 x 6 cm patch of scales on paper first (the
textures from [lesson 09.3](../lessons/module-09/lesson-03.md)), then on your forearm. Then paint the face in a mirror.

## Variations

- **Fire dragon:** red, orange and yellow scales with bronze horns and a black liner flick.
- **Ice dragon:** white, pale blue and silver scales with pale blue horns; a thin white layer under
  everything on deep skin.
- **Mermaid:** the same scale band in aqua, teal and purple, with shells instead of horns and pearls
  instead of spikes.
- **Glitter dragon:** cosmetic glitter gel on the outer scales only, at the temple and jaw, far from
  the eyes ([lesson 06.2](../lessons/module-06/lesson-02.md)).

## You're done when

- [ ] Your planner shows the features, color story and mood words before you started.
- [ ] Scales sit only in the band from forehead to jaw and fade softly into the skin.
- [ ] The horns look solid: dark side, light side, ridges and a clean outline.
- [ ] Both sides match, and the eye area has only eye-safe liner.
- [ ] Someone can say "dragon" in one second from three meters, and you photographed it.
