# Module 1 printables

Every sheet comes in A4 and US Letter, as PDF (to print) and SVG (to view or edit).

| Sheet | What it is for | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| `label-sort` | One row per product: does it say face paint, does it list ingredients, and is it for skin, the eye area, or not for skin | 01.2 |
| `consistency-test` | One row per color: paint a too-wet, a just-right and a too-dry line, and note how many drops of water "just right" needed | 01.4 |
| `clean-up-record` | The module practical: record your arm patch, how you took it off, and tick the clean-up checklist | 01.5 |

Module 1 also uses these shared sheets from `labs/common/` (they come with every module download):

| Sheet | What it is for | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| `swatch-card` | One box per color: a swatch, a line and a dot, and how much water it needed | 01.1, 01.4 |

The patch test log is a page in the course: `templates/patch-test-log.md` (lesson 01.3). Copy it
into a notebook if you have no printer.

## How to print

1. Choose **100%** or **Actual size** in the print window, never "Fit to page".
2. Measure the line at the bottom of the printed page. It must be exactly 10 cm.
3. Print on thick paper or card if you can. Tip: slide a sheet into a clear plastic sheet protector,
   paint on the plastic, wipe it clean and paint again.

No printer? Draw the same table or boxes by hand on plain paper.
