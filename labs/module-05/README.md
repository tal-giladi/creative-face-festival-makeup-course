## How to print

1. Choose **100%** or **Actual size** in the print window, never "Fit to page".
2. Measure the line at the bottom of the printed page. It must be exactly 10 cm.
3. Print on thick paper or card if you can. Tip: slide a sheet into a clear plastic sheet protector,
   paint on the plastic, wipe it clean and paint again. This works especially well for the layouts
   and eye designs.

No printer? Draw an oval face with a center line, brows and eyes on plain paper, or draw the outline
freehand.

The eye designs sheet is bigger than life size, for practice on paper. On your face, use only
products whose label allows eye-area use.
