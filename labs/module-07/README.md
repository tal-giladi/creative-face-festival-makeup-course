## How to print

1. Choose **100%** or **Actual size** in the print window, never "Fit to page".
2. Measure the line at the bottom of the printed page. It must be exactly 10 cm.
3. Print on thick paper or card if you can. Tip: slide a sheet into a clear plastic sheet protector,
   paint on the plastic, wipe it clean and paint again. This works especially well for the mandala
   grids, the wave drill and the project sheets.

No printer? Trace a cup for mandala circles and fold the paper in quarters for the spokes, or draw an
oval face with a center line, brows and eyes, or draw the outline freehand.

The red dotted ovals on the face sheets mark the eye area. On your face, only products whose label
allows eye-area use go there: no neon or UV paint, no glitter and no gems.
