## Making the stencils

> [!CAUTION]
> Cutting is a job for an adult: use small scissors or a craft knife on a cutting mat, away from the
> face. Smooth any sharp corners. Stencils never go over the eyes.

1. Print `stencil-cutouts` and cut out one card along its dashed edge.
2. Tape the card on top of a piece of thin, clear plastic: a plastic folder or clean food packaging
   that bends easily and lies flat.
3. Cut out the dark shapes through both layers. A hole punch makes the small circles on the lace card.
4. Remove the paper, wash the stencil with soap and water, and dry it between paper towels.
5. After every use: soapy water, rinse, press dry. Keep it for face paint only.

## How to print

1. Choose **100%** or **Actual size** in the print window, never "Fit to page".
2. Measure the line at the bottom of the printed page. It must be exactly 10 cm.
3. Print on thick paper or card if you can. Tip: slide a sheet into a clear plastic sheet protector,
   paint on the plastic, wipe it clean and paint again. This works especially well for the value
   ladder and illusion outlines.

No printer? Draw the boxes, shapes and face outlines by hand on plain paper, or draw the outline
freehand.
