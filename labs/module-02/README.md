# Module 2 printables: stroke drills

Each drill sheet has blocks of three rows:

1. **Trace**: paint right over the gray shapes. The dark dot shows where the brush starts and the
   small arrow shows which way to pull.
2. **Copy**: look at the gray shape at the start of the row and paint your own from each light start
   dot.
3. **On your own**: paint the same shape in the empty row without any help.

Every sheet comes in A4 and US Letter, as PDF (for printing) and SVG.

| Sheet | What it is for | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| `drill-1-pressure-strokes` | Straight, curved, down and S pressure strokes (thin, thick, thin) | 02.1 |
| `drill-2-teardrops-petals` | Teardrops in two directions, curved teardrops, five-petal flowers | 02.2 |
| `drill-3-swirls-spirals` | Curls turning right and left, spirals, S swirls | 02.3 |
| `drill-4-dots` | Even dots, dot trails, three dot sizes, dot flowers | 02.4 |
| `hand-sampler` | The Module 2 practical: a sampler to trace on a hand outline, and an empty hand to plan your own | 02.5 |

Module 2 also uses the shared `hand-arm` and `swatch-card` sheets in `labs/common/` (they come with
every module download).

## How to print

1. Choose **100%** or **Actual size** in the print window, never "Fit to page". The gray shapes are
   drawn at the real size of a stroke from a size 2-4 round brush.
2. Measure the line at the bottom of the printed page. It must be exactly 10 cm.
3. Print on thick paper or card if you can. Face paint works on printer paper too if you don't
   over-wet it.

**Sheet-protector trick:** slide a printed sheet into a clear plastic sheet protector and paint on
the plastic. Wipe it clean with a damp cloth and paint the same drill again, as often as you like.

No printer? Draw a few rows of pencil lines on plain paper and paint your strokes along them.
