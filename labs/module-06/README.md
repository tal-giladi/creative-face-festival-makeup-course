# Module 6 printables: glitter, gems and neon

Three placement maps show where glitter, gems and neon may go on the face, with the **no-go zones
shaded in red hatching**. A label check sheet records the glitters and glues you own, and two design
sheets show the Module 6 projects in light gray lines to paint over. Every sheet comes in A4 and US
Letter, as PDF (for printing) and SVG.

| Sheet | What is on it | Lesson and project |
|---|---|---|
| `glitter-label-check` | A table: product, sold for skin, ingredient list, what the flakes are made of, eye warning, patch test, use it? | 06.1 (and 06.3 for glues) |
| `glitter-map` | Face with the glitter zones (cheekbones to temples, hairline and parting, forehead center) and the no-go zones (eyelids, lash line, under the eyes, lips) | 06.2, Project 8 |
| `gem-map` | Face with dotted gem guide lines (above the brows to the temples, forehead center, top of the cheekbones) and the no-go zones | 06.3, Project 8 |
| `neon-map` | "Where neon may go": forehead, temples, cheeks and jaw, and the larger no-go eye area (brows, lids, under the eyes) | 06.4, Project 7 |
| `design-p07-neon` | Project 7: neon ribbons, sun-burst and dot trails | 06.4, Project 7 |
| `design-p08-glitter-gems` | Project 8: glitter crescents, gem arches and forehead jewel | 06.2, 06.3, Project 8 |

Module 6 also uses the shared sheets in `labs/common/` (they come with every module download):
`face-front-open-eyes` for your own designs and `face-map` for planning.

## How to print

1. Choose **100%** or **Actual size** in the print window, never "Fit to page".
2. Measure the line at the bottom of the printed page. It must be exactly 10 cm.
3. Print on thick paper or card if you can.

**Sheet-protector trick:** slide a printed sheet into a clear plastic sheet protector and paint on
the plastic. The gray lines show through. Self-adhesive face gems also stick to the plastic, so you
can practise gem lines and peel them off again. Wipe the plastic clean with a damp cloth and repeat.

Glitter on paper is hard to clean up: practise the glitter shapes with a pencil or face paint on the
sheet, and practise real glitter on your forearm (lesson 06.2).

No printer? Draw an oval face with a pencil, mark the eyes, and shade a ring around each eye as the
no-go zone before you plan anything else.
