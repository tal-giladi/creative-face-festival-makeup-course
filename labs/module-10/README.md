# Module 10 printables (optional module)

Module 10 is optional; the core course is complete without it. Every sheet comes in A4 and US
Letter, as PDF (to print) and SVG (to view or edit).

| Sheet | What it is for | Lesson or project |
|---|---|---|
| `face-to-body-plan` | A small face, a hand and forearm, and a shoulder and arm side by side, with lines for motifs, flow lines, colors, tools and where clothes touch | 10.1 |
| `vine-bracelet` | The back of the hand and forearm with the vine bracelet in light gray to trace: a bracelet round the wrist, a flower on the hand and a vine that spirals down the arm | 10.2 |
| `collarbone-necklace` | Neck, shoulders and collarbones with the center line, and the collarbone necklace with shoulder wings in light gray to trace | 10.3 |
| `arm-shoulder-piece` | The right shoulder and whole arm with the Project 13 flower vine in light gray to trace | P13 |

Module 10 also uses these shared sheets from `labs/common/` (they come with every module download):

| Sheet | What it is for | Lesson |

| `hand-arm` | A plain hand and forearm for your own bracelets and vines | 10.1, 10.2 |

## How to print

1. Choose **100%** or **Actual size** in the print window, never "Fit to page".
2. Measure the line at the bottom of the printed page. It must be exactly 10 cm.
3. Print on thick paper or card if you can. Tip: slide a sheet into a clear plastic sheet protector,
   paint on the plastic, wipe it clean and paint again.

No printer? Trace around your own hand and forearm on paper, or draw the neck, shoulders and
collarbones from a mirror, or draw the outline freehand.

On your skin, use only patch-tested face and body paint, and paint only skin you are comfortable
showing.
