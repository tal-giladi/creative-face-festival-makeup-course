# Module 4 printables: design sheets

Each design sheet is the practice face with one finished design drawn in **light gray lines**. Paint
right over the lines: sponge the big areas first, then the shapes, then the outline and white
details, in the order the lesson shows. Every sheet comes in A4 and US Letter, as PDF (for printing)
and SVG.

| Sheet | What is on it | Lesson and project |
|---|---|---|
| `design-1-flowers` | A flower crown with a vine down one temple, and a cheek flower cluster on the other side | 04.1, Project 3 |
| `design-2-rainbow-stars` | A rainbow with clouds and raindrops on one cheek, a star swoosh on the other | 04.2, 04.4, Project 1 |
| `design-3-butterfly` | The full-face butterfly on a face with closed eyes: wings, body, antennae, veins, teardrops | 04.3, Project 2 |
| `design-4-tiger` | Tiger stripes, white areas, muzzle and nose | 04.5, Project 4 |
| `design-5-hero` | A hero eye mask around open eyes and a crown with jewels on the forehead | 04.6, Project 5 |

Module 4 also uses the shared sheets in `labs/common/` (they come with every module download):
`face-front` and `face-front-2up` to paint your own versions with no lines, `face-front-open-eyes`
for masks, and `face-map` to plan where a design goes.

## How to print

1. Choose **100%** or **Actual size** in the print window, never "Fit to page".
2. Measure the line at the bottom of the printed page. It must be exactly 10 cm.
3. Print on thick paper or card if you can. Face paint works on printer paper too if you don't
   over-wet it.

**Sheet-protector trick:** slide a printed sheet into a clear plastic sheet protector and paint on
the plastic. The gray lines show through. Wipe it clean with a damp cloth and paint the design again,
as often as you like, in different colors.

No printer? Draw an oval face with a pencil, mark the center line, the eyes and the mouth, and sketch
the design lightly from the lesson pictures.
