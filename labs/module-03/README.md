# Module 3 printables

Every sheet comes in A4 and US Letter, as PDF (to print) and SVG (to view or edit).

| Sheet | What it is for | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| `color-mixing-chart` | A mixing grid for yellow, red, blue, white and black (each circle is the mix of its row and column color), and tint-and-shade ladders | 03.1 |
| `gradient-practice` | Six boxes for two-color sponge gradients, with the overlap zone marked, and an oval to fade into the paper | 03.2 |
| `pop-practice` | A heart, star, circle, leaf, flower and teardrop to fill, outline thin-thick and highlight, with the light direction marked | 03.3 |
| `symmetry-practice` | Four shapes to mirror across a center line, then half a design on a face to finish on the other side | 03.4 |
| `palette-cards` | Six cards: tick the palette type, paint the swatches and fill the 60-30-10 bar | 03.5 |
| `three-palettes` | The module practical: the same small flower design three times, one per palette | 03.5 |

## How to print

1. Choose **100%** or **Actual size** in the print window, never "Fit to page".
2. Measure the line at the bottom of the printed page. It must be exactly 10 cm.
3. Print on thick paper or card if you can. Tip: slide a sheet into a clear plastic sheet protector,
   paint on the plastic, wipe it clean and paint again. This works especially well for the
   symmetry and pop practice sheets.

No printer? Draw the same grid, boxes or shapes by hand on plain paper, or draw the outline freehand.
