# 05.3 · The festival face map: symmetrical layouts

Intermediate · 55 minutes. Festival and trance faces almost always follow a few classic layouts:
arcs from the brows to the temples, a line along the cheekbones, a "third eye" on the forehead, a
crown along the hairline, a touch on the chin. In this lesson you learn these five zones, how to
place a design so it lifts the face, and how to sketch it lightly first. You finish with three
complete layouts on the face map.

## How it works

The **festival face map** adds five zones to the face map you know from
[lesson 03.4](../module-03/lesson-04.md):

1. **Brow-to-temple arc:** from above the inner brow, over the brow, round to the temple.
2. **Cheekbone line:** from under the outer eye, along the top of the cheekbone, up toward the
   temple.
3. **Third eye:** a small spot on the center line of the forehead, just above the brows. One gem
   shape, a drop or a small sun goes here.
4. **Hairline:** a band along the top of the forehead, for crowns and rays.
5. **Chin:** a small accent on the center line.

A good festival layout uses **two or three zones**, not all five: one "star" zone with the biggest
shape, and one or two small accents. Zones 1, 2 and 4 come in mirrored pairs; zones 3 and 5 sit on
the center line and are painted last.

**Placement that lifts the face:** shapes that follow the cheekbone and sweep **up and out** toward
the temples make the face look lifted and awake. The same shapes placed low on the cheeks, or
pointing down toward the jaw, make it look tired and droopy. A quick guide: picture a line from the
corner of the mouth through the outer corner of the eye. Keep the main shapes along or above that
line, and point teardrop tails up and out.

**Sketch first, with light color.** A bold design is hard to move once it is painted. Mark the
landmarks with tiny dots of a light color, join them with a thin light line on one side, then the
other, and check from a distance. Only then paint over the sketch in bold color, one element at a
time on both sides.

## Step by step

1. **Pick a star zone** and one or two accent zones on the festival face map.
2. **Dot the landmarks** with a light color: inner and outer brow, outer eye corner, cheekbone,
   temple, forehead center. Same points on both sides.
3. **Sketch one side** with a thin light line that joins the dots.
4. **Sketch the other side** straight away, starting at the same landmarks.
5. **Check from a distance**: step back from the mirror or take a photo. Fix the sketch now if one
   side is higher or bigger.
6. **Paint the star element** in bold color on one side, then the same element on the other side.
7. **Add the smaller elements**, both sides each time, smallest last.
8. **Finish on the center line**: the third eye and the chin accent go last.

## Practice exercise

**Lift or drag?** (15 minutes), on the `face-front-2up` sheet from
[labs/common](../../labs/common/) or two ovals on paper.

1. On the left face, paint four teardrops and a few dots along the cheekbones, tails pointing up and
   out toward the temples, on both sides.
2. On the right face, paint the same shapes low on the cheeks, tails pointing down.
3. Draw the guide line (mouth corner through outer eye corner) on both faces with a light color.
4. Prop the sheet up and look from two meters away.

Stop when you can see the lift on the left face and the droop on the right, and your left-face
shapes sit along or above the guide line on both sides.

<details><summary>Hint</summary>

Teardrops pointing the wrong way? Say it from the middle: "head near the nose, tail toward the
temple". It is true on both sides, so the two sides mirror correctly.

</details>

## Mini-project

Paint **three festival layouts** on the `festival-layouts` sheet: A temple arcs, B cheekbone line and
C crown. Use your palette from lesson 05.1, sketch each one lightly first, and paint element by
element on both sides. Use the fourth face to design your own layout from two or three zones. Then
paint your favorite on your face in a mirror.

You're done when:

- [ ] Each layout has one clear star zone and no more than two small accents.
- [ ] Every element on the right sits at the same landmark as on the left, the same size.
- [ ] Shapes on the cheeks and temples sweep up and out, not down.
- [ ] The third eye and chin accents sit exactly on the center line.
- [ ] Nothing is painted inside the eye area on the face map.

## Common beginner mistakes

| What happens | Why | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Face looks tired or droopy | Shapes placed low on the cheeks or pointing down | Follow the cheekbone; point shapes up and out toward the temple |
| Design looks busy and crowded | All five zones used at once | One star zone plus one or two small accents |
| One side higher than the other | Painted without sketching, by eye | Dot the landmarks and sketch both sides lightly first |
| Sketch lines still show through | Sketch too thick or too dark | Thin lines in a pale color; paint just over them |
| Third eye slightly off center | Painted before the side elements | Paint the center line last, checking the brows and nose |

## Optional challenge

Design a **fourth layout that is not symmetrical** but still balanced: a big crown on one side of
the hairline flowing down to the cheekbone, with a small accent on the opposite temple. Sketch it on
the blank face first. Does it still feel balanced from two meters away?
