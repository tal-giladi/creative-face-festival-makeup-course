# 10.3 · Shoulders and upper body

Intermediate · 50 minutes. This optional lesson finishes Module 10; the core course is complete
without it. You will place designs on the shoulders and collarbones with the body's own guide lines,
paint a collarbone necklace with shoulder wings, and learn to keep body art comfortable, private and
off your clothes.

## Materials

> [!WARNING]
> Same rules as on the face: only patch-tested face and body paint, cosmetic glitter and skin-safe
> adhesives ([lesson 01.3](../module-01/lesson-03.md), [lesson 06.2](../module-06/lesson-02.md)), and
> never over broken, irritated or **sunburned** skin; shoulders burn easily, so skip painting after a
> day in the sun. Glitter on the chest and shoulders stays well below the face, and you close your eyes
> when you tap off loose flakes. Stop and wash off at once if the skin itches, stings or turns red.

## How it works

**Use the body's guide lines.** The upper body has its own face map
([lesson 03.4](../module-03/lesson-04.md)). The **center line** runs from the middle of the neck down
the chest. The **collarbones** are the two long bones you can feel under the neck; between them, on
the center line, is a small dip called the **notch**. The **shoulder caps** are the round tops of the
shoulders. A design that hangs from the collarbones, centers on the notch and ends on the shoulder
caps looks placed on purpose.

**Necklaces and wings.** A painted **collarbone necklace** follows the collarbones down to the notch,
like real jewelry, with a pendant on the center line. **Shoulder wings** are feather or petal strokes
that fan out from the necklace over each shoulder cap. Both are symmetrical: paint one guide dot on
each side first, check them in the mirror, then paint both sides together, step by step, as on the
face.

**Painting your own shoulders.** The front is easy in a big mirror: you can reach both collarbones,
and you reach across the body to paint the **opposite** shoulder cap (right hand to left shoulder).
Rest your elbow on your other arm or a table, and paint looking at the skin itself where you can, using
the mirror for checking. The back of the shoulders and the shoulder blades are hard to reach: ask a
willing friend or family member if you like, or simply skip them. A helper is always optional.

**Comfort and privacy.** Paint only skin you would normally show: shoulders, collarbones and upper
arms, above the neckline of the top you will wear. Practice wearing a strapless or wide-neck old top,
or with a towel, in a private room. Nobody needs to see or touch more than you are comfortable with,
and you can stop at any time.

**Clothes and paint.** Face paint dries to the touch but still rubs off when something brushes
against it, and some bright colors may stain fabric (Snazaroo). So keep designs **between the
straps** and **above the neckline**, let the paint dry for several minutes before you dress, and wear
an old or dark top over fresh paint. A light setting spray or powder helps it last
([lesson 05.2](../module-05/lesson-02.md)).

**Taking it off.** The shower is the easiest place. Wet the skin with warm water, wash with mild soap
and your hands or a soft cloth, and use a gentle oil cleanser for strong colors or glitter, then wash
again ([lesson 01.5](../module-01/lesson-05.md)). Don't scrub hard. If a top got stained, rinse the
spot in cold water and wash it with soap straight away; some pigments need more than one wash.

## Step by step

1. **Choose your top** and put on an old one for painting. Tie back long hair.
2. **Find the guides:** feel your collarbones and the notch; look at the center line in the mirror.
3. **Guide dots:** a light dot at each end of the collarbones, one on the center line just below the
   notch, one on each shoulder cap. Check that both sides match.
4. **Chain:** a dotted curve from each outer dot down to the center, with drops hanging from it,
   longer toward the middle. Paint both sides step by step.
5. **Pendant:** a teardrop on the center line, tail up to the chain, with a rim in the chain color.
6. **Wings:** four or five teardrop feathers fanning from the end of the chain over each shoulder cap.
   Paint the opposite shoulder with your painting hand reaching across.
7. **Details:** white lines on the feathers, a highlight on the pendant, dot trails.
8. **Dry and set:** let it dry for several minutes, set it lightly, then dress.
9. **Remove** in the shower with warm water, mild soap and an oil cleanser.

## Practice exercise

**Template, then one side on you** (25 minutes).

1. On the `collarbone-necklace` sheet in a sheet protector, trace the necklace and wings once, then
   paint it again on the plain `upper-body` template from memory (12 minutes).
2. In front of a mirror, find your own guides and paint the guide dots (3 minutes).
3. Paint only the chain and the wing on the shoulder that is **opposite** your painting hand
   (10 minutes). Wash it off.

Stop when the chain on your skin sits along the collarbone and the wing ends on the shoulder cap,
not under a strap or the neckline.

<details><summary>Hint</summary>

Mirror confusing you? Paint the chain with the mirror only for checking: look down at your
collarbone while you paint the dots, then look up to see if it is level.

</details>

## Mini-project

**A shoulder-to-collarbone piece on the template**, then on yourself if you like. Paint the full
collarbone necklace with both wings on the `upper-body` template in your own three colors. If you
want to, paint it on your own collarbones and shoulders in the mirror (or with a willing helper), take
a photo, and wash it off in the shower. For a bigger final piece that combines the whole module, go on
to [Project 13 · Arm-and-shoulder piece](../../projects/p13-body-art-piece.md).

You're done when:

- [ ] The pendant sits on the center line, just below the notch.
- [ ] The chain follows the collarbones and both sides match.
- [ ] The wings end on the shoulder caps, between the straps and above the neckline.
- [ ] You only painted skin you were comfortable showing.
- [ ] The paint was dry before you dressed, and you removed it with soap and an oil cleanser.

## Common beginner mistakes

| What happens | Why | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| The necklace hangs crooked | No guide dots, or each side painted freehand | Dots at both collarbone ends and on the center line first; check in the mirror |
| One wing is much smaller | The far shoulder is hard to reach | Paint the opposite shoulder first while you are fresh; rest your elbow; check with the camera |
| Paint on your top or strap | Design under a strap, or dressing too soon | Keep designs between straps and above the neckline; let it dry, then dress |
| The design smears by the afternoon | Sweat and rubbing on the chest | Thin layers, a light setting spray or powder, and blot sweat, don't wipe |
| Skin is red after removal | Scrubbing hard | Soap and an oil cleanser, a soft cloth, no scrubbing |
