# 07.4 · Planning and building a complete festival look

Intermediate · 100 minutes. A complete festival look is more than a nice design: it has a plan, a
palette, an order of work, a timetable, and it has to survive a hot day of dancing and come off
gently at night. In this lesson you plan a whole look with the design plan template, paint it in the
right order, photograph it in daylight and under UV light, and pack what you need to keep it fresh.
It is the module's final practical.

## Materials

> [!CAUTION]
> Everything in this look must be sold for skin and patch-tested
> ([lesson 01.3](../module-01/lesson-03.md)). Around the eyes, only products whose label allows
> eye-area use, nothing on the waterline ([lesson 05.4](../module-05/lesson-04.md)). **No neon, UV
> or glow-in-the-dark paint near the eyes** ([lesson 06.4](../module-06/lesson-04.md)). Only
> cosmetic glitter and skin-safe adhesives, never on the eyelids or near the lash line
> ([lesson 06.2](../module-06/lesson-02.md), [lesson 06.3](../module-06/lesson-03.md)). Never shine a
> UV torch into anyone's eyes. If skin stings, itches or an eye waters, stop and take it off.

## How it works

**Plan on paper first.** A festival look uses everything you know, so decide before you paint:

1. **Brief:** where, how long, how hot, and will there be UV lights?
2. **Palette:** two or three colors plus black and white; one main, one second, one accent.
3. **Face-map sketch:** one **star zone** (the biggest part, for example a forehead mandala or temple
   swirls) and one or two accents from the festival face map ([lesson 05.3](../module-05/lesson-03.md)).
   Mark the eye area and keep it for eye-safe makeup only.
4. **Order and timing:** write each step and its minutes.

**The order of work** is always the same: prep the skin, light sketch, big color shapes and bands,
black linework, dots and white details, eye makeup, setting, and **glitter and gems last**: they
need dry, finished paint under them, and spraying or powdering over them would wet the glitter base,
dull the sparkle and loosen the gems.

**Timing.** A full look takes beginners 60-100 minutes. Add 15 minutes for mistakes, finish an hour
before you leave so the setting can firm up, and do the eye makeup when your hand is warmed up.

**Durability.** Heat, sweat and hugs are what ruin festival faces. Prep and set as in
[lesson 05.2](../module-05/lesson-02.md), keep linework off the parts that rub most (the sides of
the nose and the jaw where a scarf or collar sits), blot sweat with a tissue instead of wiping, and
carry a small touch-up kit in the shade (FDA advises not storing cosmetics above 85 °F, about
29 °C).

**Check photos.** Take three: a daylight close-up (are the lines clean?), a daylight photo from about
three meters (does the design read as clear shapes?) and, if you used UV paint, one under a UV torch
in a dark room (does only what you planned glow, and is the eye area dark?).

**Removal at the end of the night** follows [lesson 01.5](../module-01/lesson-05.md) and Module 6:
gems lifted from the edge after softening it with oil, glitter dabbed off with tape, then an oil
cleanser and warm water and soap, and an eye-safe remover around the eyes, wiping away from them. Never sleep in it.

## Step by step

1. **Plan** on the `festival-look-plan` sheet: brief, palette, sketch, parts, order, minutes.
2. **Check products:** every one patch-tested and sold for skin; eye-safe ones for the eyes; UV
   paint only outside the eye area.
3. **Prep:** clean skin, a light moisturizer, wait until it feels dry. Contact lenses in now.
4. **Sketch** the star zone and accents lightly on both sides; check from a distance.
5. **Big color shapes** and bands, both sides each time (lessons 07.1-07.3).
6. **Black linework** over the edges, then **dots and white details**.
7. **Eye makeup:** eye-safe liner and mascara, outside the lash line.
8. **Set** with your eyes and mouth closed; wait 10 minutes.
9. **Glitter and gems last**, outside the eye area and away from the lash line.
10. **Photos:** daylight close-up, daylight from 3 m, and UV if you used UV paint.
11. **Pack the touch-up kit**; at night, remove everything gently.

## Practice exercise

**Plan and time a look on paper** (30 minutes).

1. Fill in the `festival-look-plan` sheet for a real or imagined event: brief, palette, a sketch with
   one star zone and two accents, the order of work and minutes for each step.
2. Paint the plan on the `face-front` sheet from [labs/common](../../labs/common/) with a timer
   running. Write the real minutes next to your planned ones.
3. Look at the sheet from three meters. Change the plan where a part doesn't read.

Stop when your plan has every step from prep to removal, the painted sheet matches the sketch on
both sides, and you know how long each step really takes you.

<details><summary>Hint</summary>

Ran out of time? Cut an accent, never the black linework or the setting. A simple design with clean
lines and a good set beats a big design that smears by noon.

</details>

## Mini-project

**Module 7 practical: a complete festival look on your own face**, planned, painted, set and
photographed. Use the plan from the practice exercise, combine at least two techniques from this
module (dots, geometric lines, waves) and follow the order of work. For guided versions, paint
[Project 6 · Festival eye](../../projects/p06-festival-eye.md) or
[Project 9 · Symmetrical trance face](../../projects/p09-symmetrical-trance.md).

You're done when:

- [ ] A filled-in design plan with palette, sketch, order and timing exists before you paint.
- [ ] The look has one clear star zone, matches on both sides and reads from three meters away.
- [ ] Only eye-safe products are in the eye area; no neon, glitter or gems near the eyes.
- [ ] Glitter and gems went on last, after setting, and the look passed a rub test on its edge.
- [ ] You took daylight photos (and a UV photo if you used UV paint) and removed everything gently at night.

## Common beginner mistakes

| What happens | Why | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Ran out of time halfway | No timing in the plan | Write minutes for each step, add 15 spare, finish an hour before you leave |
| Glitter dulled or slid off | Glitter put on before the setting spray | Set first, glitter and gems last |
| Design melted by midday | Skin not prepped or design not set; sweat wiped | Prep and set (05.2), blot sweat, touch up from the kit |
| Looks great close up, messy from far away | Too many small parts, no star zone | One star zone, one or two accents, black linework |
| Eye area glows under UV | UV paint placed too close to the eyes | Plan UV parts on the forehead, temples and cheekbones only |

## Optional challenge

Design a **day-to-night look**: plan which parts are ordinary paint (for daylight) and which are UV
paint (for the night stage), so the look is complete in daylight and changes under black light.
Sketch both versions side by side on one plan.
