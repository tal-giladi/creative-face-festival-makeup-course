# 09.2 · Decorative masks

Advanced · 80 minutes. A masquerade mask is the most elegant thing you can paint: a shaped band of color around the eyes, shaded so it seems to sit on the face, edged with lace and covered in fine gold scrolls. In this lesson you plan the shape, paint it with 3D shading, add lace and filigree, and finish with gems, all while keeping the eyes themselves bare and safe.

## Materials

> [!CAUTION]
> A mask goes **all around the eyes**. Every paint in it must have a label that allows **eye-area
> use**; believe a "not for use near the eyes" label even if the box photo shows otherwise (FDA).
> Paint only up to the edge of a wide almond around each eye: never on the lash line or waterline.
> **No neon, UV or glow paint, no glitter and no gems near the eyes**: flakes and stones can fall in
> and scratch the eye (American Academy of Ophthalmology). Gems go on the forehead and the temple
> tips only ([lesson 06.3](../module-06/lesson-03.md)). Patch-test everything first
> ([lesson 01.3](../module-01/lesson-03.md)).

## How it works

### The shape

A **masquerade mask** is one symmetrical shape ([lesson 03.4](../module-03/lesson-04.md)) built from
the hero mask of [lesson 04.6](../module-04/lesson-06.md), but more graceful: it dips at the center
between the brows, covers the brows, rises to a point (or a curl, or a wing) at each temple, and
curves under the eyes onto the top of the cheekbones. The **eye holes are wide almonds**, bigger than
the eye, so a ring of bare skin always stays between the paint and the lashes. That gap is both the
safety rule and the thing that makes a mask look elegant.

### 3D shading

A flat color looks like a sticker. A mask looks lifted off the face when you use the light-and-shadow
idea from [lesson 08.2](../module-08/lesson-02.md):

- **Base:** sponge the mid color, a little lighter at the top and darker at the bottom.
- **Rim shadow:** a darker shade of the same color sponged softly **just inside every edge**: around
  the outside and around both eye holes.
- **Light bands:** a soft lighter stripe over each brow and along the curve of the cheek, where light
  would hit a raised surface.
- **Cast shadow (optional):** a very soft, thin line of a darker skin-tone or brown just below the
  lower edge, as if the mask threw a shadow.

### Lace and filigree

**Lace** is a row of small scallops along the lower edge with a dot in each and tiny dots outside
([lesson 08.3](../module-08/lesson-03.md)). **Filigree** is fine metalwork: thin lines that curl. You
paint it with a liner and three moves: the **C-scroll** (a line that curls into a spiral), the
**S-scroll** (two curls joined, turning opposite ways) and small **teardrops and dots** at the joins.
Paint one side, then copy it on the other: filigree only looks rich when it is mirrored. A thin dark
line under gold (or a darker mask color) makes it shine.

## Step by step

1. **Check labels.** Every paint in the mask is eye-safe and patch-tested. Gems only for the forehead and temples.
2. **Plan.** With a thin, light line, dot the center and both temple tips at the same height. Sketch the outline and a **wide almond** around each eye.
3. **Base.** Sponge the mid color inside the outline, lighter at the top, darker at the bottom. Eyes closed for the brow part, looking up for the cheek part. Stay outside the almonds.
4. **Shade.** With a clean corner of sponge, dab the dark shade just inside every edge. Soften it inward. Dab a lighter tint over the brows and along the cheek curve. Let it dry.
5. **Outline.** Black or a very dark shade with the liner, in short strokes: the outside edge, then the almonds. Keep the almond line on the mask, never on the bare skin by the lashes.
6. **Lace.** Along the lower edge: scallops first, then a dot in each, then tiny dots outside. Or sponge white through a lace stencil held on the cheek, well below the eye.
7. **Filigree.** On one side: a long vine along the brow band ending in a curl, two small curls branching up, a curl rising to the temple tip, and a vine along the cheek band. Copy it on the other side, then add teardrops and dots.
8. **Finish.** White highlight dots and a sparkle on the light bands. Gems last: one above the center, one at each temple tip.

## Practice exercise

**Filigree and lace on paper, then one mask on the template** (40 minutes).

1. On the `filigree-drill` sheet: trace each gray model, then paint it twice beside it. Do the lace
   row to the end. 10 minutes.
2. On the `artistic-mask` sheet in a sheet protector: paint the mask with base and shading only.
   Wipe it and repeat until the shading looks soft and the edges are dark. 15 minutes.
3. Paint it once more with lace and filigree on both sides. 15 minutes.

Then paint one filigree vine on the back of your hand. Stop when your curls end in clean points and
both sides of the mask match.

<details><summary>Hint</summary>

Turn the sheet (or your hand) so you always pull the brush toward yourself, and load only the tip.
For the mirrored side, paint the matching curl first at its end point, then join it back: matching
end points is easier than matching whole lines.

</details>

## Mini-project

**A masquerade mask on your own face** (in a mirror), after painting it on the template. Choose a
shape from the demonstration or design your own with the thumbnails of
[lesson 09.1](lesson-01.md). For a fully guided version, paint
[Project 11 · Artistic mask](../../projects/p11-artistic-mask.md).

You're done when:

- [ ] Every product near the eyes is eye-safe, and a clear almond of bare skin surrounds each eye.
- [ ] The mask has a gradient base and 3D shading: darker edges and light bands.
- [ ] Lace and filigree are thin, clean and mirrored on both sides.
- [ ] Gems are only on the forehead and temple tips; no glitter or gems near the lashes.
- [ ] You took a photo, then removed it gently, eyes closed, wiping away from the eyes.

## Common beginner mistakes

| What happens | Why | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Paint creeps onto the lashes | Eye holes sketched too small | Sketch wide almonds first; fill only outside them |
| Mask looks like a flat sticker | One flat color, no shading | Dark shade inside every edge, light band over the brows |
| Filigree looks blobby | Too much paint, pressing too hard | Load only the tip, light pressure, lift at the end of each curl |
| One side's filigree looks different | Painted freehand on both sides | Copy curl by curl: match the end points first |
| Gems fall off or eyes water | Gems or glitter too close to the eyes | Gems on forehead and temples only; remove anything near the eye |

## Optional challenge

Paint a **half mask**: only one side of the face, sweeping up into a big swirl or wing at the temple,
with the other side bare except for a small echo of filigree. Asymmetric masks look dramatic in
photos; keep the eye area rules on both sides.
