# 09.4 · Designing your own artistic look

Advanced · 120 minutes. This is where you stop following designs and start making your own. You learn a simple loop that working artists use: write a brief, gather references without copying, sketch, paint, check yourself honestly and try again, then photograph the result for your portfolio. The lesson ends with your first original design, which you finish as Project 12.

## Materials

> [!WARNING]
> An original design can go anywhere on the face, but the safety rules don't change: only products
> sold for skin, patch-tested ([lesson 01.3](../module-01/lesson-03.md)); in the eye area only
> eye-safe products, and no glitter, gems or neon paint near the eyes
> ([lesson 05.4](../module-05/lesson-04.md), [lesson 06.4](../module-06/lesson-04.md)). Never paint
> over irritated or broken skin, and take the design off before you sleep.

## How it works

### Start with a brief and constraints

A **brief** is two or three lines that say what the design is for: "A dreamy night look for a
party, about four hours, one side only." Add **constraints**, the limits you choose on purpose: how
long you have to paint, which colors you own, where it will be seen (photo, party, festival). A
blank page is hard; a brief with constraints makes choices easier.

### References, not copies

A **reference** is a picture you learn from. Collect three or four, but **take one idea from each**:
the colors of a sunset sky, the shape of a moon, the pattern of a piece of lace. Then mix them your
way. Copying someone else's whole face design is fine for practice at home (that is how the
projects in this course work), but it is not your original design, and you should never present it
as yours. If one picture gives you the whole design, add more references until you have to make
choices. The same respect from [lesson 09.3](lesson-03.md) applies: no sacred or cultural designs
worn as a costume.

### Iterate and self-check

Your first version is a draft. Paint it on a template, look at it with the
[Self-check](../../templates/self-check.md) from one meter and close up, and pick **one** box you
could not tick. That is the goal for version 2. Change only that, paint again, check again. Two or
three versions usually turn a busy idea into a clear one. Thumbnails from
[lesson 09.1](lesson-01.md) are version zero.

### Photograph and keep a portfolio

A **portfolio** is a collection of your best finished work. For each design keep one page: a
front photo from about one meter, a close-up of the best part, the brief, the palette, what worked
and what you will change. Take photos the same way every time: daylight from a window in front of
you, a plain background, the camera at eye level. Then you can see your progress honestly.

## Step by step

1. **Brief.** Write two or three lines: what, where, how long. Add constraints: time, colors you own, one side or both.
2. **References.** Collect three or four pictures. Next to each on the `mood-palette` planner write the **one** thing you take from it.
3. **Features and palette.** 3-5 features as simple shapes, and a color story with mood words (lessons 09.1 and 09.3).
4. **Thumbnails.** Three on the `idea-thumbnails` sheet; distance test; choose one.
5. **Plan.** Place it on the `face-map` and fill in the [Design plan](../../templates/design-plan.md), including the products check.
6. **Version 1 on a template.** Paint it in a sheet protector, in your planned order.
7. **Self-check.** Go through the [Self-check](../../templates/self-check.md). Pick one goal.
8. **Version 2.** Change only that goal. Repeat until the self-check is mostly ticked.
9. **Paint it on your face**, then photograph: front from one meter, a close-up, same light and background.
10. **Portfolio.** Fill in the `portfolio-page`: photos, brief, palette, what worked, next time.

## Practice exercise

**Brief to version 2, on paper** (50 minutes).

1. Pick one constraint card: "only three colors", "one side only", "no brush wider than size 2", or
   "a theme from the weather". Write a brief that uses it. 5 minutes.
2. Collect three references and write what you take from each. 10 minutes.
3. Three thumbnails, distance test, choose one. 15 minutes.
4. Paint version 1 on a `face-front` sheet from [labs/common](../../labs/common/), self-check it,
   write one goal, paint version 2. 20 minutes.

Stop when version 2 is clearly better than version 1 at the goal you chose, and you can say in one
sentence what changed.

<details><summary>Hint</summary>

Most first versions have **too much**. If you can't decide what to change, remove the smallest
third of the elements and look again from a distance.

</details>

## Mini-project

**Your first original design**, from brief to portfolio page. This mini-project is the start of
[Project 12 · Your original face design](../../projects/p12-original-design.md): do the brief,
references, thumbnails, plan and at least two template versions here, then paint it on your face
and finish the portfolio page in the project.

You're done when:

- [ ] Your brief and constraints are written, and each reference has one idea next to it.
- [ ] You drew three thumbnails and chose one with the distance test.
- [ ] You painted at least two versions on a template, each with a self-check and one goal.
- [ ] The final plan keeps the eye area for eye-safe products only.
- [ ] You know which version you will paint on your face for Project 12.

## Common beginner mistakes

| What happens | Why | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Stuck at a blank page | No brief or constraints | Write a brief and pick one constraint first |
| The design looks just like someone else's | One reference used for everything | Take one idea from each of 3-4 references |
| Each version is completely different | Changing everything at once | Change only the one goal from the self-check |
| Photos make the design look worse than it is | Mixed light, busy background, flash | Window light in front, plain background, same distance every time |
| Not sure whether it is finished | No way to judge | Self-check from one meter and close up; if most boxes are ticked, it is done |

## Optional challenge

Make a **series**: three original designs on one theme (for example the four seasons, or three
moons: new, half, full), sharing one palette and one texture but with different layouts. Put the
three portfolio pages side by side.
