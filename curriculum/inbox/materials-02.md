## Dotting tools

**What they are for:** round dots, dot trails and dot flowers (lesson 02.4).

**Ideal:** a double-ended dotting tool (a small stick with a metal ball of a different size on each end).

**Cheaper or easier to find:** a nail-art dotting tool; the round end of any brush handle (medium dots); the tip of a round brush (tiny dots); a new cotton swab (big, soft dots).

**A substitute must:** have a smooth, round end with no sharp point; be washable (or new and used once, like a cotton swab); and be used only for face paint. Never a pen, toothpick or craft tool.
