- **flow line** — the main curve that carries a design and that the other shapes follow, for example a vine spiraling down the arm (10.1)
- **landmark** — a part of the body you can see or feel and use to place a design, such as the wrist bone, the shoulder cap or the collarbones (10.1)
- **non-dominant hand** — the hand you don't write with; you paint on it with your dominant hand (10.2)
- **bracelet** — a painted band that goes all the way round the wrist, just above the wrist bone (10.2)
- **wrap** — a vine or band that spirals around the arm, leaving one edge and coming back on the other (10.2)
- **shoulder cap** — the round top of the shoulder, a good place for the biggest motif of a body design (10.3)
- **collarbones** — the two long bones you can feel at the base of the neck; necklace designs follow them (10.3)
- **notch** — the small dip between the collarbones, on the center line (10.3)
- **collarbone necklace** — a painted chain that follows the collarbones down to a pendant on the center line (10.3)
- **shoulder wings** — feather or petal strokes that fan out from a necklace over each shoulder cap (10.3)
