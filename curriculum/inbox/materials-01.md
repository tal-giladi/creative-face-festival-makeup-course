# Materials additions from Module 1

## Cotton pads and cotton buds

**What they are for:** removing paint with micellar water or an oil cleanser (pads), especially
around the eyes; putting a small patch-test spot on the arm (buds).

**Ideal:** plain cotton pads and cotton buds.

**Cheaper or easier to find:** a clean, soft cloth cut into squares (wash after each use); a clean
brush for the patch-test spot.

**Must:** be clean and soft, used once, and never shared between people for the eyes.

## Towels

**What they are for:** a clean surface under the kit, covering clothes, drying skin and tools.

**Ideal:** two clean old towels: one under the kit, one for drying.

**Cheaper:** a clean tea towel; paper towels for drying.

**Must:** be freshly washed before each painting session.

## Bowl for used sponges

**What it is for:** keeping used sponges apart from fresh ones, so a used sponge never goes back
into the paint (lesson 01.3).

**Ideal:** a small bowl or tray.

**Cheaper:** a clean cereal bowl or a plastic lid.

**Must:** be clearly separate from the fresh sponges.

## Petroleum jelly (eye-area removal)

**What it is for:** a gentle way to take paint off around the eyes when you have no eye-safe remover
(the American Academy of Ophthalmology suggests it for eye makeup).

**Must:** be plain and fragrance-free, and stay out of the eye itself.
