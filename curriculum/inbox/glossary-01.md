- **label** — the printed text on a product or its pack, including the product type, the ingredient list and any warnings (01.2)
- **ingredient list** — the list of everything a cosmetic is made of, on the pack or the seller's page; colors appear in it as CI numbers or color names (01.2)
- **CI number** — Colour Index number, a code such as CI 77891 that names one color ingredient on a cosmetic label (01.2)
- **CosIng** — the European Commission's free online database of cosmetic ingredients, where you can look up a CI number (01.2)
- **eye area** — the brows, eyelids and the skin just under the eyes; only products whose label allows it may go there (01.2)
- **fluorescent (neon)** — very bright colors that glow under a UV black light; none are allowed near the eyes (01.2)
- **glow-in-the-dark** — paint that shines in the dark after light has charged it; only for occasional use and never near the eyes (01.2)
- **non-toxic** — a label on art materials meaning safe for art use as intended; it does not mean safe for skin (01.2)
- **black henna** — a dark "henna" sold for temporary tattoos that often contains PPD and can cause blisters, scars and lifelong allergy; never use it (01.2)
- **PPD** — para-phenylenediamine, a hair dye chemical found in "black henna" that can cause serious skin reactions (01.2)
- **kohl** — a traditional eyeliner (also kajal or surma) that often contains lead; avoid it (01.2)
- **patch test** — painting a small spot of a new product on the inner forearm and watching it for 24-48 hours before using it on a face (01.3)
- **allergic reaction** — redness, itching, swelling or a rash because the body reacts to one ingredient (01.3)
- **irritation** — redness or stinging because a product is too harsh for the skin, even without an allergy (01.3)
- **hygiene** — keeping hands, tools and the work space clean so germs don't pass from one person to the next (01.3)
- **consistency** — how thick or runny the paint is; "just right" is creamy, like melted ice cream (01.4)
- **activate** — wetting a paint cake so its top layer turns into creamy, usable paint (01.4)
- **load** — picking up paint with a brush or sponge (01.4)
- **swatch** — a small filled square of one color, painted to see how it looks and behaves (01.4)
- **swatch card** — a sheet with one box per color: a swatch, a line, a dot and a water note (01.4)
- **dab** — pressing a sponge onto the skin and lifting it straight off, instead of wiping (01.4)
- **micellar water** — a gentle cleansing water used on a cotton pad to lift makeup and strong face paint colors (01.5)
- **oil cleanser** — a gentle oil-based face cleanser that dissolves cream or oil-based paint; eye-safe versions can be used around the eyes (01.5)
- **ferrule** — the metal part of a brush that holds the hairs (01.5)
