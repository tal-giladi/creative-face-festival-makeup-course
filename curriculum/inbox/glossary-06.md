- **cosmetic glitter** — glitter sold for face and body, with an ingredient list; the only glitter that goes on skin (06.1)
- **craft glitter** — glitter sold for cards, slime and decorations; not made or checked as a cosmetic, often with bigger, sharp-edged flakes; never on skin (06.1)
- **bio glitter (plant-cellulose glitter)** — glitter made from a film of plant cellulose instead of plastic; lower-impact, but not harmless to nature (06.1)
- **cellulose** — the material plant walls are made of; used to make plant-based glitter flakes (06.1)
- **microplastics** — tiny pieces of plastic, such as plastic glitter flakes; the EU restricts adding them to products on purpose (Regulation 2023/2055) (06.1)
- **PET (polyethylene terephthalate)** — a common plastic; a glitter that lists it in its ingredients is plastic glitter (06.1)
- **glitter base** — a thin, slightly sticky skin-safe layer (cosmetic glitter gel, aloe vera gel, or a little petroleum jelly on the body) that glitter is patted onto so it stays put (06.2)
- **glitter roots** — glitter along the parting of the hair and the hairline (06.2)
- **brow bone** — the bony ridge under the eyebrow hair, above the eye; gem lines follow it above the brow (06.3)
- **self-adhesive face gem** — a flat-backed gem with skin-safe glue already on the back, sold on a sticker sheet (06.3)
- **spirit gum** — a skin adhesive for bigger pieces on the face and body; flammable until dry, needs its own remover, never near the eyes (06.3)
- **prosthetic adhesive** — a strong skin adhesive (for example Pros-Aide) for bigger pieces, away from the eyes (06.3)
- **graded line** — a line of gems or dots that gets smaller step by step from one end to the other (06.3)
- **fluorescent (neon, UV-reactive)** — color that turns invisible ultraviolet light into bright visible color, so it glows under a black light; never near the eyes (06.4)
- **black light (UV torch)** — a lamp that gives off ultraviolet light (often 365 nm) and makes fluorescent colors glow; never shone into anyone's eyes (06.4)
- **glow-in-the-dark** — paint that stores light and glows after the lights go out; in the US only luminescent zinc sulfide, for occasional use, never near the eyes (06.4)
- **Special FX (SFX) paint** — paint sold for props, fabric or effects that is not a cosmetic; never on skin (06.4)
