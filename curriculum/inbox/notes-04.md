# Module 4 notes (for the main session)

- Technique sources (checked 2026-10-07): Snazaroo "Butterfly face paint 3 easy steps" and "Tiger face paint 3 easy steps" (uk.snazaroo.com, via search index; the tiger page says to sponge the base over the face "taking care not to go too close to the eyes"), Snazaroo application and removal page, FacePaint.com Teardrops 101 (as in Module 2). No new safety or regulatory facts: all safety lines follow references/safety.md and research/safety-sources.md (eye-area paint only if labeled for the eyes; believe "not near the eyes" labels; no neon near the eyes; no glitter or gems near the lash line, AAO; self-adhesive gems on forehead/cheekbone; never superglue or school glue).
- Design decision: the tiger puts white over the brows (not on the eyelids) and is painted with open eyes, so it needs no eye-safe paint on the lids. The butterfly is the only Module 4 design that paints the eyelids (eyes closed, eye-safe paint); the cheek butterfly is the alternative when paint is not eye-safe. Masks (04.6, P5) need eye-safe paint around the eyes and leave a bare-skin gap at the lashes.
- Skin tones: 04.1 fair, 04.2 brown, 04.3 light, 04.4 tan, 04.5 medium (cat) and deep (tiger), 04.6 fair; P1 medium, P2 deep, P3 brown, P4 light, P5 tan.

The sheets import LINEART from assets-m04.mjs, so running sheets-m04.mjs also regenerates the Module 4 assets.
