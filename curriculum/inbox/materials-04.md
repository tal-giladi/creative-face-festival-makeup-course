## Rainbow split cake (optional)

**What it is for:** one-stroke rainbows and petals: several colors loaded onto a flat brush at once (lesson 04.4 optional upgrade; Module 8).

**Ideal:** a face-painting split cake (several colors side by side in one cake) and a wide flat brush, 1.5-2.5 cm.

**Cheaper or easier to find:** skip it and paint rainbows stripe by stripe with a round brush from your normal colors.

**A substitute must:** be sold as face paint. Never load a brush from craft paint, poster paint or watercolor sets, even for stripes.
