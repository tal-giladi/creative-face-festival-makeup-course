- **saturated color** — the pure, strong version of a color, like hot pink rather than baby pink (05.1)
- **color blocking** — painting flat areas of solid color side by side, each with a clean edge, instead of blending them (05.1)
- **complementary pop** — a small touch of the opposite color next to a big area of the main color, which makes both look stronger (05.1)
- **linework** — thin black (or white) lines between and around color areas that keep each color clear (05.1)
- **neon look** — bright colors placed next to black and white so they seem to glow, without UV paint (05.1)
- **beading** — water-based paint pulling into little drops on oily skin or wet cream instead of a smooth layer (05.2)
- **primer** — an optional thin skin product put on before paint or makeup to help it go on smoothly and last (05.2)
- **setting spray** — a spray misted over finished makeup or face paint to help it resist rubbing and sweat (05.2)
- **translucent powder** — a colorless face powder pressed over makeup or paint to set it and soak up shine (05.2)
- **rub test** — pressing a tissue on a painted, set patch and rubbing it gently to see how well it lasts (05.2)
- **touch-up kit** — a small bag with what you need to fix a design during the day (05.2)
- **festival face map** — the face map with five festival zones: brow-to-temple arc, cheekbone line, third eye, hairline and chin (05.3)
- **third eye** — a small design on the center line of the forehead, just above the brows (05.3)
- **star zone** — the one main area of a layout with the biggest shape; the other zones are small accents (05.3)
- **eye area** — the skin from the brows down to the top of the cheekbones, where only eye-safe products may go (05.4)
- **lash line** — the edge of the eyelid where the lashes grow; eye-safe liner goes here, on the outside (05.4)
- **waterline** — the wet inner rim of the eyelid, inside the lashes; no makeup goes here (05.4)
- **graphic wing** — eyeliner along the upper lash line that flicks out to a sharp point (05.4)
- **crease** — the fold of the upper eyelid that appears when the eye is open (05.4)
- **floating crease** — a line painted just above the natural crease so it shows with the eyes open (05.4)
- **brow bone** — the slightly raised bone just under the eyebrow (05.4)
- **open-eye design** — an eye design painted with the eyes open, made to be seen that way (05.4)
- **closed-eye design** — an eye design painted on the moving lid, seen mainly when the eyes are closed (05.4)
