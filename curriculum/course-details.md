## One-line summary

Paint flowers, butterflies, festival looks and fantasy faces from your very first brushstroke, with
cheap skin-safe materials, printable practice faces and no model needed.

## Who it is for

Complete beginners with no makeup, art or drawing experience who want to paint fun faces for kids'
parties, festivals, raves, costume events or just for themselves. You practice on paper, printed
face templates and your own hand and arm before you ever paint a face, so you don't need a model.
A small budget kit is enough; every item has cheaper substitutes.

## Format

9 short modules in 3 levels plus an optional body-art module: 44 lessons, each ending in something
you paint, 13 step-by-step projects (rainbow cheek to your own original design), a multiple-choice
quiz after every lesson and module, printable practice faces (front, half face, eye area, face map,
hand and arm, upper body; A4 and US Letter), a materials and substitutes guide and a safety guide.
Self-paced; most lessons take 5-10 minutes to read and 20-45 minutes to practice.

## Prerequisites

None. A printer helps (for the practice faces) but is not required: every template can be traced or
drawn freehand.

## What you will learn

- Choose skin-safe face paints, glitter, gems and adhesives, and know what must never go near the eyes.
- Patch-test, keep your kit clean and remove every product gently.
- Control a brush and sponge: pressure strokes, teardrops, swirls, dots and smooth gradients.
- Mix colors from a few paints and pick color combinations that always look good.
- Paint symmetrical designs using a simple face map.
- Paint classic designs: flowers, stars, butterflies, rainbows, animals and hero masks.
- Build complete festival and trance looks with bold color, glitter, gems and safe neon/UV paint.
- Turn your own idea into an original fantasy or artistic face design (and, optionally, body art).
