# Course outline (master list)

Not imported. The lesson titles here are the master titles: `_sidebar.md` and every H1 use them
exactly. Lesson ids are `NN.M`. Difficulty is the first word of each lesson's intro paragraph.

Course: **Creative Face & Festival Makeup — From First Brushstroke to Artistic Designs**
Repo: `tal-giladi/creative-face-festival-makeup-course` · slug `face-festival-makeup` · free.

## Level 1 — Creative Face Painting

### Module 1 — Tools, Materials and Safety
| Id | Title | Level | Hands-on result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01.1 | Your face painting kit | Beginner | A kit list with a substitute chosen for every item you don't have |
| 01.2 | Skin-safe or not? Reading the label | Beginner | Sort products at home into skin-safe / eye-safe / not for skin |
| 01.3 | Patch test and clean hands | Beginner | A patch test on your inner arm, logged; a clean setup |
| 01.4 | Water, paint and your first swatches | Beginner | Swatch card: the right paint consistency for every colour |
| 01.5 | Taking it off and cleaning up | Beginner | Paint a small patch on your arm, remove it, clean the kit (module practical) |
